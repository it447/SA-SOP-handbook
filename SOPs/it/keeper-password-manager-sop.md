## Goal

Scale Army uses [Keeper](https://www.keepersecurity.com/) as the company's official password manager. This SOP covers who has access to Keeper, how it's used to store and share credentials, and who to contact when you need a password or access to a shared vault record.

## Who this is for

All Scale Army team members who need access to shared company credentials (client portals, internal tools, vendor accounts, etc.).

## What Keeper is used for

- Storing and sharing credentials for company tools, client platforms, and vendor accounts.
- Giving individuals or teams scoped access to only the credentials they need, instead of passwords being shared over Slack, email, or other insecure channels.
- Keeping an auditable record of who has access to what.

## Who has access

Access to Keeper is granted on a need-to-know basis by IT. Not everyone at Scale Army has a Keeper seat by default — access is provisioned per role, and shared folders/records within Keeper are scoped so a given team member only sees the credentials relevant to their work.

## Requesting a password or access

If you need a password, or need access to a credential you don't currently have in Keeper:

1. **Reach out to Seif (Seif Farid)** — the primary point of contact for all Keeper access requests and password needs.
2. If Seif is unavailable, **reach out to Joel** as the backup.
3. If Joel is also unavailable, **reach out to Yos (Yosele Angulo)** as the second backup.

Do not ask around informally or request credentials be shared outside of Keeper (e.g. over Slack DM or email) — all password sharing should go through Keeper so access stays tracked and auditable.
