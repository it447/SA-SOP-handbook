- **ATS (Applicant Tracking System):** Software used to manage and track candidates throughout the recruitment process.
- **BE (Backend):** Server-side systems handling data processing, logic, and integrations — e.g. building APIs, processing form submissions, handling authentication, syncing data between systems (like HubSpot ↔ database). Used by Backend Developers and DevOps/IT.
- **Benchmarking:** Comparing compensation or practices against market standards.
- **Candidate Experience:** The overall perception and experience of a candidate during hiring.
- **Compensation (Comp):** Total pay structure, including base pay, bonuses, and stipends.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to labor laws, regulations, and company policies.
- **Cultural Fit:** The alignment between a talent's values/behaviors and company culture.
- **Disciplinary Process:** A structured process to address performance issues or policy violations.
- **Deel:** A global HR platform used to manage contracts, payroll, time off, and compliance for international talent.
- **Feedback:** Constructive input provided to improve performance and development.
- **FX Policy (Foreign Exchange Policy):** Defines how exchange-rate fluctuations are handled for international compensation.
- **Google Workspace:** Company communication/productivity tools — email accounts, Drive file sharing, Google Meet, document access management. Used company-wide.
- **Headcount:** The total number of talent or contractors within the organization.
- **IRS (Internal Review Sheet):** Recruiter-created document with detailed candidate info, used for internal assessments.
- **IT:** Information Technology — maintains, develops, and implements technology systems.
- **JD (Job Description):** A detailed description of a role's responsibilities and requirements.
- **LOI (Letter of Intent):** A formal document outlining a candidate's intended agreement (start date, compensation).
- **MSA (Master Service Agreement):** The comprehensive agreement between Scale Army and a client, defining the relationship's terms.
- **Margin:** The fee added on top of a candidate's salary expectation, usually starting from $1,199.
- **Net Compensation (NTC):** What a talent earns after taxes, benefits, and other payroll deductions.
- **Net Salary:** Take-home pay after all deductions.
- **Onboarding / Offboarding:** Integrating new hires into the organization / formally managing a talent's exit.
- **PTO (Paid Time Off):** Paid leave for vacation, illness, or personal reasons.
- **PIP (Performance Improvement Plan):** A formal plan to help a talent improve within a defined timeframe.
- **Payroll:** The process of paying talent their compensation and wages.
- **Retention:** Strategies and practices aimed at keeping talent within the organization.
- **SOP (Standard Operating Procedure):** A documented step-by-step process for completing tasks.
- **Stakeholders:** Individuals or groups with an interest in or influence over a process or decision.
- **Stipends:** Non-wage compensation provided in addition to salary.
- **Screening:** Reviewing resumes, applications, and other materials to evaluate candidate suitability.
- **Talent:** Refers to contractors working with the organization.
- **Talent Lifecycle:** The stages a contractor goes through, from recruitment to offboarding.
- **Talent Engagement:** The level of commitment, motivation, and emotional connection a talent has with the company.
- **Turnover:** The rate at which talent leaves the organization.
