## Goal

Explain how to write a Standard Operating Procedure at Scale Army, so every SOP follows the same structure and stays easy to find and maintain.

## Who this is for

Anyone at Scale Army who is creating or editing an SOP.

## Every SOP must contain

- **Goal:** what the SOP explains or accomplishes.
- **Who this is for:** the audience/role(s) the SOP applies to.
- **Other Link:** a link to any supporting doc, spreadsheet, or Loom walkthrough.
- **Checklist:** the concrete steps to follow.
- **Who has contributed to this:** the people who wrote/maintain it.
- **When was this last updated:** the most recent update date.

## Checklist for creating a new SOP

1. Copy the template.
2. Fill it out completely.
3. Refresh the Table of Contents.
4. Check the heading formatting to make sure it didn't break anything.

## Note

After adding a new SOP to the master handbook doc, scroll to the top and refresh the Table of Contents to confirm headings are structured correctly.
