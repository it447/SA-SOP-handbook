## Goal

A single master index of every external link (Google Doc, Sheet, Drive folder, Ashby dashboard, Slack thread, or contact email) referenced throughout the master "Scale Army SOP Handbook" document, organized by department. Extracted directly from the handbook's actual hyperlinks (not just the visible link text), so the assistant can answer "where's the link for X" with the real destination URL.
