## Purpose

Give marketers, sales team members, and anyone working with the funnel a shared, precise vocabulary for how a contact moves from a cold lead toward a qualified sales opportunity, plus a quick overview of which channels currently drive marketing results. The goal is alignment — when someone says "MQL" or "meeting held," everyone means the same thing.

## Who this is for

Marketing Team, Sales Team, and anyone reading a marketing report or dashboard.

## Part 1: Top-of-funnel stage definitions

The sequence a contact moves through, from giving an email to an AE determining whether they're a real sales opportunity.

### 1. Lead

A contact who has given an email address but hasn't fully converted or booked a meeting. Because they've only shared minimal info, they're enrolled into email (and sometimes SMS/text) nurture cadences designed to move them toward booking a meeting — not yet treated as sales-ready.

> Key idea: a lead has given an email address, and nothing more — no phone number, no meeting on the calendar.

### 2. Meeting Booked

The first full conversion — the contact provides a phone number and qualifying details (e.g. which role they're hiring for) and actively schedules a meeting, currently via the Calendly pop-up connected to HubSpot. This scheduling event is tracked as the first meaningful sales event in the funnel. From this point, the contact gets a sequence of SDR follow-up emails to confirm and prepare for the call.

> Key idea: meeting booked = email + phone + qualifying details + an actual date on the calendar.

### 3. MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)

Two related but distinct versions:

- **Contact MQL** — the lightweight, broader definition: qualifies simply by not being spam, not a competitor, and not a duplicate record.
- **MQL (full definition)** — the stricter version: must have already booked a meeting, have a valid company domain (not free/personal email), not be a competitor, and clear the restricted business list check.

Every full MQL is a meeting booked that's passed extra quality checks; "Contact MQL" is the lighter early filter that strips obvious junk before deeper qualification.

### 4. Meeting Held

The contact actually speaks with an Account Executive (AE) — the scheduled meeting takes place. This is the point the contact transitions from a marketing-managed record to an active sales contact. Once it happens, the AE tags the contact with two qualification fields (SAL and SQL) to determine whether it was a good opportunity.

## Part 2: Marketing channel overview

Where current marketing volume and results come from:

- **Paid media (dominant channel):** over 80% of marketing results by volume. Within that, Meta (Instagram + Facebook combined) accounts for over 95% of paid media volume — by far the primary paid channel today. Smaller tests are running on X (Twitter), Google Ads, and LinkedIn, with YouTube planned.
- **Organic search:** traffic primarily through Google, driven by SEO and content work on landing pages and articles.
- **Organic social:** results mainly from Instagram.
- **Emerging/experimental channels:** early tests on ChatGPT ads — still experimental, not yet a core reliable channel.
