## Goal

A comprehensive guide for onboarding and training the sales team, equipping Account Executives (AEs) with the tools and knowledge to sell Scale Army's services and drive growth.

## Who this is for

Sales Team, Sales Managers, new hires/onboarding, and Leadership.

## 1. Macro concepts to understand

- **Deck:** The presentation shown on sales calls and sent to prospective clients.
- **Generic Sheet:** An example candidate sheet with top candidates, shared with clients to set expectations on the process.
- **Engagement Letter (EL):** Sent to the client to initiate a hiring process.
- **MSA:** The main service agreement.
- **Deposit tiers:** Several deposit amounts exist depending on the offer type — an early-bird rate for signing within 48 hours on one role, a regular per-role deposit, and several kickoff-deposit tiers used for different promotions. Check the current Stripe links internally before quoting, since these change.

## 2. General standards

- Sales calls can be scheduled with as little as 2 hours' notice, unless doing so would leave the AE under 30 minutes to prep for their next booked call, or conflicts with a blocked lunch that can't be moved.
- 1 hour of admin time per day (Mon–Fri) is allowed outside pre-requested blocks.
- Every AE has individual, personal performance goals.
- Outbound calling runs continuously in the background between booked calls.
- The current script/call structure must be followed on every call.
- Every technical role requires a secondary call with the AM.
- Every quote must land between "Acceptable" and "Hero" on the pricing calculator.
- 100% of calls are recorded (Fathom or Google Meet).

## 3. The sales call process

### Pre-call prep
- Research the company/business model (enrichment tooling, AI-assisted research, website, LinkedIn).
- Check the intake form / ad source.
- Create a custom proposal deck with the deposit link ready for signature and payment.
- Prep 2–3 adjacent case studies.

### Tabs to have open during every call
Sales script, proposal deck, case studies, the Generic Sheet (show tabs similar to the role the client is hiring for), the pricing calculator, the scoping questions sheet, personal messaging, and a notetaker (e.g. Fathom).

### Call #1
- Join 1 minute early.
- Follow the current sales script exactly.
- Have a note-taking template ready.
- Reference the Objections/Rebuttals doc as needed.

**Make sure to:**
- **Set clear expectations** — be explicit about deliverables, timelines, and results.
- **Set clear next steps** at the end of every call (follow-up call, proposal, or JD).
- **Add the client to a Slack Connect channel** at the end of the call.

**Avoid:**
- Discussing pricing before presenting value.
- Asking the same question twice.
- Leaving a client question unanswered — if you don't know, say you'll find out and follow up.
- Sharing information irrelevant to the client's actual needs.
- Raising concerns the client hasn't mentioned.

**Common failure patterns to avoid** (from call review): lack of clarity/consistent messaging, saying "one-time price" when the engagement is ongoing (always clarify pricing structure), being unprepared for process questions, and sounding unsure — speak with confidence, backed by facts.

### Booking the follow-up (when needed)
Required when the role is technical, the decision-maker wasn't on the call, or the prospect needs more time.
- Book live, on the call — never "I'll send you a link."
- Same day or next day; sooner is always better.
- Get a live "yes" on the calendar invite before ending the call.
- Send a short follow-up email covering pain points and potential solutions.
- Send a day-of confirmation text; if no response, follow up with a simple "?"

### Call #2
- Reference the Call #2 SOP before any sales call involving a potential technical role.
- Minimum 30 minutes.
- AE preps that morning with details shared in the "Two-Step Sales Process" Slack channel.
- Sync with the AM for 5 minutes before the call.

### Post-call
1. Confirm the SDR marked the meeting instance complete in HubSpot (Demo Meeting Completed / Completed / No Show).
2. Rename the deal to "Company Name – Role Name."
3. Update the deal stage in HubSpot to reflect the outcome (Create JD, Temp Hold, Links Sent, Closed Lost TOF, etc.).
4. Update MQL, SAL, SQL, and attribution at the contact level.

**If moving to "Create JD":**
- Draft the JD using personal notes + call transcript against the standard JD Template.
- Double-check the JD format before sending, and give the client comment-only access.
- Create the proposal (ask Dijah or Elena about the Qwilr walkthrough if needed).
- Send the paperwork with the Next Steps email template in HubSpot.
- Once the deposit is signed, confirm the client accepted the Slack Connect invite (resend if needed).
- Log the meeting in your personal tracker.

**If moving to "Temp Hold":**
- Send the initial Temp Hold follow-up template right after the call.
- Never wait more than 5 days to follow up — sooner is always better.
- Create HubSpot tasks matching any commitments made to the client (follow-up date, sending example candidate videos, etc.).
- Every Temp Hold deal must have follow-up activity scheduled in HubSpot.

### Launching a search
1. Launch the search for candidates per the standard walkthrough.
2. Introduce the client to their assigned AM using the provided template.

### When a lead doesn't show up
1. Wait 2 minutes before taking action.
2. Text and email the lead (e.g. "Hey [Name], [rep] here with Scale Army. Are you joining our call? Hope everything's okay.")
3. After 5 minutes, try calling them.
4. Stay on the call up to 8 minutes total before dropping.
5. Mark the deal No Show: move the record from Meeting Scheduled → Closed Lost TOF, reason "No Show."

## 4. Outbound motion

- Roughly 100 cold calls/day and 10 cold LinkedIn touches/day per rep.
- Outbound runs continuously in the background between booked inbound calls.
- See the Outbound Cold Calling SOP / Playbook for scripts.
