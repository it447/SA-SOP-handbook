- **Deck:** The presentation shown on the sales call and sent to prospective clients.
- **Generic Sheet:** An example candidate sheet with top candidates, shared with clients on the sales call to set expectations on the process (a sneak peek at quality/process).
- **EL (Engagement Letter / Proposal):** The letter or proposal sent to initiate a hiring process. It must be signed to launch a search.
- **MSA (Master Service Agreement):** The main service agreement sent once a candidate is placed successfully; must be signed to initiate the ongoing monthly engagement.
- **Deposit:** The amount required to kick off the search process. Getting the deposit paid and the EL signed is an AE's primary goal.
