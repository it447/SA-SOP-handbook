## Principle

A deposit not paid within 72 hours of a call is usually lost to momentum — the follow-up cadence below exists to prevent that drop-off.

## Tone rules

- Confident, witty, warm. Assume the deal is happening.
- Keep every touch short — each one needs a reason to exist.

## Ownership

- **AE = the face.** Every text, email, and call goes out from the rep's own account.
- **SDR = backstage.** Builds the custom website and any personalized assets, maintains templates, and hands them to the rep ready to send.
- Once the Engagement Letter (EL) is sent, the automated follow-up sequence turns on. If the deal moves out of that stage, the contact is automatically unenrolled. Tasks are created for each deal owner and must not be ignored.

## The cadence

| Day | Channel | Owner | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 (within 1 hr) | Text + Email + LinkedIn request, live on the call | AE | Confirm the proposal was sent, express excitement to launch the search, and share the EL/deposit link (should take ~2 minutes to complete). |
| Day 1 | Email + Call/Voicemail | AE | Check for JD/proposal questions; remind them the early-bird offer expires end of day tomorrow if still applicable. |
| Day 2 (no deposit) | Text + Call | AE (automated text assist) | Confirm the link came through; note that the deposit increases if the early-bird window has closed. |
| Day 3 | Email + LinkedIn | AE | Light check-in: "are you getting my emails? Hope everything's okay," plus a LinkedIn note confirming you're ready to help once they have what they need. |
| Day 5 | Text + custom website | SDR builds, AE sends | AE sends a personalized website built for that company/role, funneling into the same deposit flow. |
| Day 7 | Slack (or LinkedIn if Slack isn't available) | AE | Check in on timeline and offer to answer anything blocking the deposit. |
| Day 10 | Calendar invite with deposit link in the description (marked Free, no meeting link attached) | AE/automated | Light, self-aware "are you ghosting me?" tone, restating you can help if they still need the hire. |
| Day 12 | Lead magnet email (new thread) | AE/automated | Share the internal step-by-step sourcing guide as a goodwill last resort. |

## Exit & kill rules

- **Explicit "no" / "not now" / "we paused" → stop immediately** and move to long-term nurture. Never run the next touch on someone who has already declined.
- **Deposit paid at any point → exit the sequence** and trigger onboarding.
- **No response after Day 12 → move to long-term nurture.**

## SDR asset checklist (build once, reuse per deal)

- Day 3 custom website template (fillable in under 10 minutes per deal)
- Day 7 personalized audio asset (5-step generation SOP)
- Voicemail scripts for Day 1 and Day 5

## Step-by-step system mechanics

- **During the call:** add the contact on LinkedIn and ask if they're on Slack.
- **The day the EL is sent:** paste the EL URL into the contact's EL Link property in HubSpot first, then move the associated deal to the "Engagement Letter Links Sent" stage so the automation fires correctly. The automation sends the first text; the AE manually sends the "kicking off your search" email.
- **Day 1:** AE attempts one call (voicemail if it doesn't connect) and sends the scripted follow-up email reminding them of the early-bird deadline.
- **Day 2:** automation sends a follow-up text; AE reaches out again by phone.
- **Day 3:** AE manually reaches out by email and LinkedIn with the scripted copy.
- **Day 5:** if the EL still isn't signed, the AE requests a custom website from the SDR team at the start of the day and sends it from the same phone line used previously.
- **Day 7:** reach out via Slack if available, otherwise LinkedIn.
- **Day 10:** if still unsigned, an automatic calendar invite with the EL link fires at 5:00pm EST (chosen to avoid disrupting AE calendars). This only works if the EL Link property was filled in on Day 0.
- **Day 12:** an automated email shares the lead magnet as a last resort. No response after this may be marked Closed Lost at the AE's discretion.

**Important:** the HubSpot workflow only continues while the deal stays in the "Links Sent" stage — as soon as the deal stage changes (Closed Lost or Closed Won), the sequence stops. Keeping deal stages updated is what turns the automation off correctly.
