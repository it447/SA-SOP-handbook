## Goal

Provide a reference for legal and HR terms, ensuring understanding and consistency across the company when communicating with contractors.

## Who this is for

HR Team, Legal Team, and Leadership.

## Why this matters

A primary distinction between employees and contractors is the degree of control over their work activities. Communicate clearly with contractors so they understand their freedom to choose when and how they complete their tasks. When engaging with contractors, make sure they're aware of their status, agree with their contractual arrangements, and understand their own responsibilities for taxes and local regulations.

## Words and phrases to avoid vs. use

Use this table for all written or verbal communication with contractors (email, Slack, or otherwise):

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Salary / wage / payroll | Payment, fees, compensation, rate, earnings, remuneration |
| Employee / staff | Contractor, consultant, freelancer |
| Promotion / career | Project extension, rate increase |
| Employee benefits / benefits | Project incentives, project conditions |
| PTO / leave / paid leave / vacation / sick leave | Time-off, unavailable hours, scheduled downtime, availability adjustment, medical absence, health break |
| Performance review / discipline | Feedback, project assessment/review |
| Working hours | Availability windows, active/available hours, project hours, work schedule |
| Position, job, employment | Role, assignment |
| Resignation / layoff / fire | Project termination, contract termination/expiration, service discontinuation, project suspension |
| Supervisor / Manager | Project coordinator, point of contact, project lead |
| Onboarding | Project initiation, project setup, engagement commencement |
| Duties | Assignments, project deliverables, tasks, responsibilities |

## What a contractor's job description should always include

- **Project Overview:** For fixed-term assignments, describe the project and key deliverables. For longer-term projects, include a detailed scope of work specifying key tasks and milestone deliverables.
- **Skills and Qualifications:** Clearly list the technical skills required to fulfill the role.
- **Project Success Metrics:** Measurable outcomes used to assess the effectiveness of the contractor's work upon completion, where applicable.

## Hardware stipend

Should be added in Deel by the contractor as an Expense — "Hardware purchase" — with a purchase receipt uploaded.
