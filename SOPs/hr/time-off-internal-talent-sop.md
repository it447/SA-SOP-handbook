**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** January 2026
**Owner:** HR Department — Scale Army
**Applies to:** Internal Talent

**Purpose:** To provide a clear process for Scale Army internal talent to request
Planned Availability Adjustments (PTO), ensuring all days are properly recorded in
Deel and communicated to the team.

## PTO Policy

- Each Talent has 25 Planned Availability Adjustment days per calendar year.
- Talent accrue 2.08 days per month from their start date (after the trial period).
- Talent can take up to 3 un-accrued days in advance; additional days will be unpaid.
- PTO allotment resets at the start of each calendar year.

## Notice Requirements by Duration

| Duration | Minimum Notice |
|---|---|
| Half-day | 48 business hours |
| 1–2 consecutive business days | 1 week |
| 3–4 consecutive business days | 1 month |
| 5 or more consecutive business days | 3 months |

## Coverage

Talent should arrange for a colleague to cover their responsibilities. Coverage
must be confirmed in writing and sent to the manager at least 5 business days in
advance.

## Company Closures

Scale Army is closed on Dec 25, Dec 31, and Jan 1. These days do not count against
PTO.

## Time Off Classification (by Hours)

To avoid confusion, time off is classified strictly by hours as follows:

### 0:00 – 2:00 hours off

- Permission-based (not counted as PTO).
- Inform your manager and your coverage (if needed).
- Block the time on your calendar and update your Slack status.
- Catch up asynchronously on your workload.
- No HR approval or Deel entry required.
