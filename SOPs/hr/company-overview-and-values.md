## What is Scale Army?

Scale Army is a staffing agency dedicated to connecting international talent with US-based companies. We cover a broad range of positions and industries, from customer support and sales roles to engineering and executive-level positions.

## Who this is for

Everyone at Scale Army — and anyone asking the chatbot what the company does or what it stands for.

## Core Values

### Easy To Work With

You're pleasant to be around. You make Scale Army a place people like showing up to. You're the kind of person others want in the room.

### Care Deeply

You care deeply about the company, the talent, the clients, and the people who work here. If something isn't right, you will push to make it so.

### Rigor

Go 10 levels deep. On your projects. On your deliverables. On your data.

### Ownership

If you see something, say something. Even when that something is you.

### Sharp

You speak quickly. You learn quickly. All the lights are on.
