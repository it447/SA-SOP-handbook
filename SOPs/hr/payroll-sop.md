## Goal

Outline the payroll process to ensure accurate and timely payments to employees.

## Who this is for

HR Team, Finance Team, and Leadership.

## Relevant Slack channels

- **internal-core-team-hr-requests** — submitted forms from internal Talent for stipends and time off.
- **internal-team-hr-finance** — upper management + HR only (except two named exceptions); used to send final screenshots and the internal crossmatch.
- **payroll-prep-hr-and-finance** — external payroll flags, shared with Finance leadership and internal HR/Finance.
- A dedicated small-group channel where the commission file, exceptions, and guarantee-commission questions are shared each cycle.

## Process

1. **Log into the Admin Deel account.** Filter by group for clarity — external talent by client (excluding the company's own internal groups), or internal talent by the internal groups.
2. **Open the relevant profile** → "Payments, expenses and work submissions" → the current active contract.
3. **Add the relevant line item:** Expense (stipends), Bonus (any extra bonus beyond monthly commissions), Commissions (monthly commissions), or Other (base pay increases/decreases).
4. **For expenses:** select "Manually Create" and fill in:
   - **Date:** the payment due date — always the 15th or 30th/31st (or the first business day before, if that date falls on a weekend/holiday), depending on the pay cycle.
   - **Category:** Wellness (gym, psychologist, nutritionist), Medical Costs (doctor visits, medicine, therapy), Coworking (coworking space), Equipment (hardware), or Work From Home (e.g. SIM subscription).
   - **Amount:** per the payroll prep sheet, checking current currency if working from scratch.
   - **Description:** format as "Name of Stipend – Reason – Month", e.g. "Lifestyle Stipend - Gym Membership - Dec 2025".
   - Submit, then use "Add More" if the same person has additional stipends, or "Close" if done.
5. **For commissions:** the commission prep sheet is shared each cycle and may include guarantee-commission or bonus exceptions requiring confirmation. Copy the shared file into the team's working Prep Sheet exactly, watching for same-day or next-day edits, then submit each commission individually per Talent. If a Talent has multiple line items (stipends, commission, bonus), submit them together to avoid repeated back-and-forth.
6. **Mark each item as completed** in the Payroll Prep Sheet once submitted to Deel.
7. **Reconcile and export:** once everything is marked complete, go to the Deel admin Finance tab, reset filters, filter by group (internal vs. external, excluding named exceptions for external), select all, and click "Pay Selected."
8. **Send a screenshot of the payment breakdown to Finance (Sandra)** for reconciliation against the payroll prep sheet, Deel, and her own tracking sheet. Flag and resolve any discrepancies.
9. **Download the CSV** (via the three-dot menu → "Download CSV" next to View Invoices).
10. **Upload the CSV** to the "HR + Finance" Drive folder, in the corresponding "Payroll 202X" subfolder, and open it with Google Sheets.
11. **Clean up the sheet:**
    - Rename it "Internal Crossmatch – Month Cycle X, 202X" or "External Crossmatch – Month Cycle X, 202X" as appropriate.
    - Delete columns A–E, then H and I.
    - Filter row 1 on "Worker Name."
12. **Assign and share:** add the responsible POC's name in column I and add checkboxes for every row. Share the sheet with the appropriate internal or external reconciliation group.
13. **Notify via Slack**, tagging the relevant POCs in `internal-team-hr-finance` (internal) or `payroll-prep-hr-and-finance` (external).
