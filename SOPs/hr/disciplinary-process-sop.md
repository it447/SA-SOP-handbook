## Purpose

The Hint (Disciplinary) System supports continuous improvement, ensures accountability, and provides fair, consistent guidance when performance or behavior concerns arise. The goal is always development first, not punishment.

## Who this is for

HR Team, Managers, and Leadership.

## What is a Hint?

A Hint is an official step in the disciplinary process — a structured feedback and correction stage designed to help a talent identify areas for improvement, understand clear expectations, and demonstrate progress.

## When the Hint system may be applied

- Performance issues (not meeting KPIs or job expectations)
- Behavioral or attitude concerns
- Communication issues
- Attendance or reliability problems
- Misalignment with company values/culture
- Policy violations

Hints may be applied individually or combined. The process is progressive, but serious violations may result in an accelerated step.

## Hint levels

| Hint Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Hint 1 – Verbal Warning | Initial formal notification and coaching conversation. |
| Hint 2 – Written Warning | Written notice outlining concerns and improvement expectations. |
| Hint 3 – Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) | Structured plan with goals, timeline, and checkpoints. |
| Hint 4 – Final Warning | Last opportunity to improve, with stricter monitoring. |
| Hint 5 – Termination | Contract ends due to lack of improvement or serious violation. |

Management and HR reserve the right to accelerate steps for severe misconduct or urgent cases.

## Validity and reset timeline

- Each Hint stays active for 6 months from the date it's successfully closed.
- If no further concerns arise within that window, the Hint clears.
- If a new issue arises within the 6-month period, the process resumes at the next relevant step.

## Support during the process

Talents receive: clear expectations and improvement goals, coaching/support from their manager and/or HR, and documentation of deadlines and progress checkpoints. The intent is to give every opportunity to succeed and improve.

## Confidentiality

Hint discussions and documents are confidential, shared only with HR, the direct manager, and leadership when required.

## Commitment to fairness

The system aims for consistency, clarity, and equal treatment, centered on support, growth, accountability, and transparency.
