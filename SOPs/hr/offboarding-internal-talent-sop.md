## Goal

Outline the offboarding process for internal talent, ensuring a smooth and respectful exit.

## Who this is for

HR Team, Account Managers (AMs), and Leadership.

## Offboarding scenarios covered

- **Failure to pass the trial period:** the talent doesn't meet expectations during trial and the contract isn't extended.
- **Underperformance after PIP/Boost plans:** the talent continues to underperform after a performance improvement or boost plan, with no observed improvement.
- **Voluntary resignation:** the talent resigns and gives notice.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Manager calibration meeting
- HR and the manager meet (via Google Meet) to discuss the reasons for offboarding.
- Gather feedback and insights from the manager — performance reviews, improvement plan outcomes, and other relevant context — so HR has enough detail to answer the talent's questions and prepare for the offboarding meeting.

### Step 2: Manager fills the offboarding form
- The manager completes the internal Offboarding Google Form. Submitting it triggers a Slack notification in the `SA-Internal-Offboarding` channel. IT and HR then offboard the talent from all tools during the offboarding meeting.

### Step 3: Prepare necessary documentation
- Gather all paperwork related to the talent's contract, payments, and benefits: final pay details, remaining vacation/leave balances, and any other owed compensation or benefits. Have these ready before the offboarding meeting.

### Step 4: Offboarding meeting with the talent
- Schedule a meeting to officially communicate the decision and gather feedback.
- During the meeting: discuss the reason for offboarding, review the talent's experience at Scale Army, and conduct an offboarding interview to gather feedback, improvement suggestions, or exit reasons.
- Document all feedback and share it with the relevant managers and HR team. Log it in the Internal Check-in tracker.

### Step 5: Send a farewell email
- Draft a farewell email: To the company-wide distribution list, CC the departing talent's personal email.
- Include a farewell message, a general (non-exposing) reason for departure, and the talent's contact details if they'd like to stay in touch.
- Send only after all feedback and offboarding steps are complete.

### Step 6: Offboard in Deel
- Search for the talent in Deel (filter by group if helpful).
- Follow the termination procedure, accounting for any outstanding payments, vacation days, or benefits in the final payout. Check "View Contract" and "Payments" — approve or deny any pending stipends/expenses before ending the contract.
- Update the contract end date:
  - If ending on a specific date, choose "End on a date" and select the final day. If ending immediately, choose "End now."
  - In the termination box, mark whether this is a termination or a resignation, and select the reason from the dropdown (with an explanation if needed).
  - Leave "Impact" empty.
  - Set "Rehire" eligibility based on the reason for leaving.
  - If the reason is not an end-of-trial, generate a termination letter for the talent to sign and attach it.
- Confirm the Deel offboarding is fully complete before moving to the next step.

### Step 7: Update the internal payroll database
- Update the internal payroll and internal-tracking spreadsheets to reflect the termination, ensuring payroll records are adjusted for the next cycle.
- Confirm the update is saved and notify relevant stakeholders (HR, payroll) of the change.
