**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** January 2026
**Owner:** HR Department — Scale Army
**Applies to:** Internal Talent to hire Internally and Externally

**Objective:** Establish clear and consistent guidelines for hiring procedures
related to high-risk industries and countries at Scale Army. This policy protects
the company's legal standing, ensures compliance with international laws and
sanctions, and supports operational feasibility while aligning with client and
talent needs.

## 1. General Principles

Scale Army is committed to engaging clients and talent only in industries and
jurisdictions that comply with legal, ethical, and operational standards.

- **Approval Requirement:** Engagements with clients in certain industries or
  sourcing talent in certain countries require explicit, written approval from
  Alex Kruger (Chief Executive Officer) or Yosele Angulo (Senior Operations
  Manager, HR & Finance) prior to any recruiting, sourcing, or contracting
  activity.
- **Contractor Nature:** All Talent are engaged as independent contractors.
  Compliance with local, US, and international trade laws remains mandatory.

## 2. Industries Requiring Approval

No recruiting or client engagement may proceed in the following restricted or
high-risk industries without written approval from Alex Kruger or Yosele Angulo:

- Gambling
- Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Healthcare (telemedicine, supplements, medical devices, etc.)
- Drugs (cannabis, psychedelics, or any federally restricted substances)
- Sex / Adult Content (OnlyFans agencies, cam sites, etc.)
- Finance (cryptocurrency, NFTs, trading platforms, investment advisors, etc.)
- Weapons, Firearms, Ammunition
- Tobacco, Vaping, E-cigarettes
- Alcohol
- Political Campaigns or Advocacy Groups
- Multi-Level Marketing (MLMs)
- Payday Loans, Debt Settlement, Credit Repair
- High-Risk E-commerce (dropshipping scams, fake reviews, copyright issues)
- Surveillance Technology, Spyware
- Any other category deemed illegal, highly regulated, misleading, or reputationally risky

> **Important:** If an opportunity is unclear, the responsible team member must
> seek clarification and approval before proceeding.

### 3.2 Countries to Avoid

Sourcing talent from the following countries is strongly discouraged and
requires written approval from Alex Kruger (CEO) or Senior Operations
Management, HR & Finance:

- Canada (only dependent contractors)
- Argentina
- United Kingdom
- Italy
- Spain

### 3.3 General Regional Note

Asia is not strictly prohibited (other than the above-listed jurisdictions), but
sourcing in Asia is generally discouraged due to time zone misalignment with
Scale Army's core client base. Teams must evaluate operational feasibility for
any hiring that may impact delivery commitments.

## 4. Process for Seeking Approval

- **Team Responsibility:** Identify any client or role falling under restricted
  industries or countries. Submit a formal written approval request to Alex
  Kruger (CEO) or Yosele Angulo (Senior Operations Manager, HR & Finance) prior
  to any sourcing, recruiting, or contracting.
- **Manager Responsibility:** Verify that approvals are documented and retained
  in the candidate or client record. Confirm compliance before moving forward.
- **Compliance Responsibility:** Confirm that engagements do not violate current
  OFAC sanctions, export controls, or any other applicable trade restrictions.

**Preferred Payment Platform:** Scale Army's standard and preferred method of
engaging and paying Talent is via Deel, to ensure compliance with contractor
status, tax requirements, and local employment laws.

Alternative payment methods, such as UpWork or direct deposit, may only be used
if there is a clear and documented business case justification. Any deviations
must be reviewed and approved by Alex Kruger (CEO) or Yosele Angulo (Senior
Operations Manager, HR & Finance) in advance and recorded in the Talent's
engagement file.

## 5. Important Notes

- No recruiting, sourcing, or contracting may proceed for restricted industries
  or countries without documented, written approval from Alex Kruger or Yosele
  Angulo.
- All approvals must be retained for audit and compliance purposes.
- Breaches of this SOP may result in contract cancellation to protect Scale
  Army's legal standing and reputation.
- This SOP will be reviewed regularly to remain up to date with applicable laws.
