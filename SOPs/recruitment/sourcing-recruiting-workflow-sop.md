## Goal

Lay out the step-by-step process for sourcers and recruiters once a new search is initiated — covering responsibilities from job creation in Ashby through to handoff for screening and eventual close (Closed Won/Closed Lost).

## Who this is for

Sourcers, Recruiters, the Sourcing Coordinator (Youssef), and Recruiting Leadership.

**Steps Overview — Sourcing Workflow:**
- Step 1: New Search Intake
- Step 2: Job Creation in Ashby
- Step 3: Sourcer Review & QA
- Step 4: Batch 0 Creation
- Step 5: Recruiter Review & AM Send-Off
- Step 6: Sourcer Pipeline Ownership & LinkedIn Job Posting
- Sourcing Quotas: Candidates to Push to Tech Screen
- Step 7: Recruiter Tech Screen Review
- Step 8: End of Sourcer's Role (per Deal)

**Steps Overview — Recruiter Workflow:**
- Step 1: Job Post Review (with Sourcer)
- Step 2: Batch 0 Review
- Step 3: AM Client Feedback
- Step 4: TRS Send-Off

- Step 10: Close the Role in Ashby

# Part A: Sourcing Workflow

## Step 1: New Search Intake

- A new search is submitted via the **New Search** Slack channel.
- It is already assigned to a POD, AM, Recruiter, and Sourcer.

**Assignment Criteria (in this order):**
1. POD
2. Capacity

## Step 2: Job Creation in Ashby

The **Sourcing Ops Specialist** creates the job on Ashby using the **External Search Master Template**. The following must be correctly filled in:

- **POD** (Internal SA, Revenue Drivers, Growth & Brand, and Product Builders)
- **Recruiter** (Account Manager)
- **Recruiting Coordinator** (Recruiter)
- **Sourcer**

Location(s) should be based on the Sourcing Strategy Criteria:
- Aim at **one** LATAM country for LinkedIn Automatic Slots.
- 3 maximum African countries.

### Step-by-step: SOP for Inbound Job Posting

**Step 1 — Access Slack's #new-searches channel:**
- Find the **#new-searches** channel on Slack and star it for easy access.
- If you can't find it, ask your senior to add you to the channel.

**Step 2 — Review the New Search Form:**
- When an AM/AE fills out the new search form, it's posted as a message in #new-searches.
- Read the message carefully for details on the client, role, salary, expectations, and any important notes.
- Visit the client's website and watch the sales Loom introducing the client. Pay attention to specific client requirements.
- Open the JD doc and thoroughly understand the role, and flag any concerns (unicorn role, salary too low, etc.) to the Sourcing Coordinator/Manager.

**Step 3 — Assign the Correct Team in Ashby:**
- Open Ashby and go to the "Admin" tab.
- Search for "Departments & Teams" and click on it.
- Expand "Scale Army Careers" and search for the client's name to check if a team has been assigned.
- If no team exists, create one by selecting "Add a team to Project Growth," typing in the client name, and hitting "Create." **Do NOT press Enter — it will not create it.**

**Step 4 — Creating the Job Post in Ashby:**
- Go to the "Jobs" tab in Ashby, select "Dashboard," and search for the "Master Template."
- Open the template and click "More" > "Copy."
- Modify the job title to follow the format: **Company Name - Role Name.**
- Add the newly created team by searching for the client name.
- Enter the compensation per month as provided in the Slack message.
- Select the correct pod based on the role.
- No need to add any more details — every other setting is added automatically.
- **Assign the hiring team:**
  - Recruiter = Account Manager
  - Recruiting Coordinator = Assigned Recruiter
  - Sourcer = Assigned Sourcer
  - (This info is in the #new-searches thread on Slack.)

**Step 5 — Prepare the job post draft:**
- Click the "Draft" dropdown under the job title and select "Mark as open."
- In the "Job Postings" tab, click the template and change the title to the **ROLE**. Do NOT add the client name.
- Fill in the social description and job description using the JD doc as a reference — ensure everything is clear, well-worded, and grammatically correct (use Grammarly).
- Under "Basic Info," add the main location and any other countries requested.

**Banned countries (never post to these):**
- North Korea
- Iran
- Cuba
- Syria
- Crimea Region, Ukraine
- China
- Saudi Arabia
- South Korea
- Japan
- France
- Germany
- United States of America
- Venezuela

Remove/add countries based on the new search requirements as well; refer to your senior if you have any questions.

**Step 6 — Create the Application Form:**
- Go to the "Admin" tab and search for "Application Forms."
- Use Ctrl+F to find the Master Application Form Template.
- Copy it and change the title to match the new role: "Client Name - Role." Make sure to group the correct client (see original Loom walkthrough for a screenshot of this step).
- Update the application questions based on the JD's qualifications and responsibilities.
  - Add required fields for all important questions.
  - Make sure the **first** app form question is "How many years of experience do you have in ___."
  - Aim for 8 questions total.
  - Questions you are **not** allowed to remove: years-of-experience question, Skills & Tools questions.

**Step 7 — Link the Application Form to the Job Post:**
- Once the application form is complete, save the changes, then change the link to the new application form on the job post (see original Loom walkthrough for a screenshot of this step).

**Step 8 — Set up Job Post Automation:**
- Go to the "Automation" mini-tab and click "Create a Configuration."
- Set the rejection conditions (Level of English, video submission) (see original Loom walkthrough for a screenshot of this step).
- Click "Activate" to turn on automation for the job post.

**Step 9 — Publish the Job Post:**
- After everything is set up, click "Publish" to post the job.

**Step 10 — Respond to the #new-searches Slack thread:**
- Reply to the thread, attaching the job post link and the application form link for the assigned recruiter to review.

## Step 3: Sourcer Review & QA

- The Sourcer checks the job post and QAs it (the recruiter also QAs it independently).
- Confirm nothing is missing: no important questions or key info.
- The **Sourcing Coordinator (Youssef)** should QA all job posts (description and app form).
- Recruiter QAs it and replies with an emoji on the thread; AM should also review it and reply with an emoji on the thread.

## Step 4: Batch 0 Creation

- The Sourcer immediately starts looking for candidates on Ashby to build Batch 0.
- **Timeline:** Batch 0 must be submitted within 24 hours.
- **Format:** use the [03_Candidates Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jkdIaOwwl1RAFCh50XVv7_7c5_KC1poJ). See the [Loom video on how to add your role](https://www.loom.com/share/0b70401e8bb14297bc53e1fae0d49d88).

> For the full Batch 0 definition, guidelines, and workflow (what it is, who does what, budget/timing rules), see [[batch-zero-process|Batch Zero Process]]. The summary below covers only how it fits into the sourcing workflow.

**Batch 0 in brief:**
- A maximum of five candidate profiles submitted to the client within 48 hours of initiating a search, to calibrate direction with the client — not a definitive shortlist.
- Candidates must have been sourced or applied within the preceding three months.
- Target confirming availability/interest of at least 50% of the candidates.
- Most candidates should fall within budget; one exceptional over-budget candidate is permissible with a clear disclaimer: *"This candidate's compensation expectations are outside your stated budget; however, we believe their exceptional experience or suitability warrants consideration."*
- Every candidate in Batch 0 must be contacted via WhatsApp/email along these lines: *"We have your profile on file and are interested in considering you for a [TITLE] role with a budget of $X. We will be sharing your profile with the client shortly. Please inform us as soon as possible if you are open to consideration. Should the client express interest, we will contact you to schedule a call. If you are not interested, please feel free to notify us."*
- Batch 0 is **not**: a real-time list of recently-applied candidates, a final interview shortlist, or optional — every search must include one.

**Batch 0 workflow (roles):**
- **Sourcer:** identifies/selects candidates, confirms availability/interest through outreach, assembles the Batch 0 deck or list.
- **Sourcing Coordinator (Youssef):** reviews the draft Batch 0, verifies availability status, sourcing recency, budget compliance, and overall alignment.
- **Account Manager (AM):** reviews the final list against the client's requirements, sends Batch 0 to the client, and follows up to solicit feedback and direction.

For the **first 3 searches** of every sourcer, Youssef should book a shadowing call with the sourcer and walk through how to build Batch 0 together. Candidates should be pushed into the **Batch 0 stage** in Ashby; the stage limit for Batch 0 is the Take-Home stage — a candidate cannot be taken for Batch 0 past that stage.

## Step 5: Recruiter Review & AM Send-Off

1. **Review of Batch 0 by Sourcing Coordinator:** the Sourcing Coordinator reviews Batch 0, ensuring all candidate profiles meet the specified requirements for the role.
2. **Folder Creation in the [Batch 0 Master Sheet](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1f_9BBvz31XqYlt8Es5f9UT71fHihlkcu):** the Sourcer uses the Batch 0 Master Folder to track and record all candidates. After reviewing Batch 0, the Sourcer creates folders for each client and role in the master Batch 0 folder on Google Drive.
3. **Client Folder Creation:** create a Client Folder within the Batch 0 directory. Naming convention: `[Client Name]` (e.g., "Scale Army").
4. **Role Folder Creation:** inside the Client Folder, create a subfolder for the Role. Naming convention: `[Role Name]` (e.g., "Sales Manager").
5. **Application Packets Creation:** in the Role Folder, create individual Application Packets for each candidate. Naming convention: `[Candidate Name]`. Each packet should contain: candidate resume, portfolio (if applicable — use the letterhead template), and candidate video.
6. **Send-off to Account Manager (AM):** once folders/packets are ready, the Sourcing Coordinator messages the pods channel to the AM with:
   - A brief overview of the candidates in the batch (name, why suggested, average salary expectations).
   - Confirmation that all candidate files are placed in the designated client and role folders.
   - A link to the role folder.
   - A flag if any candidate is out of budget, and how.
7. **AM's next steps:** the AM reviews the candidate packets, assessing suitability for the client's needs, and communicates with the Sourcing Coordinator if any further clarification or adjustments are needed.

## Step 6: Sourcer Pipeline Ownership & LinkedIn Job Posting

Even before Batch 0, sourcers should always monitor their pipeline — especially within the first 48 hours of posting — tracking quantity & quality of applicants and applications for live roles.

For roles that require **outbound** efforts, sourcers can use our LinkedIn corporate account. Before messaging anyone, polish your profile: profile picture, header, location, and set your current job to "Sourcer at Scale Army."

If you find good candidates, have them apply to the job (must submit the application form) using **your personal tracking link**. **Do NOT create a candidate manually on Ashby** — see the [Loom video on this](https://www.loom.com/share/e53df5fda72b4b5fa0e9ae21aea953f8).

### SOP: LinkedIn Job Posting

**Create a Project on LinkedIn Corporate Account:**
- Log into the LinkedIn corporate account and create a new job project for the client or role.
- Ensure job title, description, and requirements are filled out accurately.
- Set the appropriate location and other job-specific details based on the client's requirements.

**Using Custom Tracking Links:**
- Open Slack to find your designated custom tracking link for the specific job posting in **#sourcing-division**. If you don't have one, ask the Sourcing Coordinator.
- Open the job posting in Ashby and copy the tracking link from Slack (assuming the sourcer's tracking code is, e.g., `SingrTrking`).
- Open the job posting application form for that job and paste the custom tracking link into the appropriate section.
- Ensure the entire link is copied — it should look like: `https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/ProjectGrowth/60f86269-d1a5-41b8-b716-5ed2d71464bb/application?utm_source=SingrTrking`

**InMailing Candidates for Apply Starter Automatic Slots:**
- Bulk InMail candidates with the application link using the custom tracking link.
- The InMail message should contain a concise version of the job description, role requirements, and the application form link with your tracking link.

**Objective:** ensure all job applications are tracked accurately using custom tracking links for each job posting.

**Step-by-step: adding your tracking link to a message template**

1. **Access the Job Posting in Ashby** — open Ashby, navigate to your job group (e.g., "E-commerce Manager"), and open the job posting.
2. **Copy the Custom Tracking Link** — open the application link, highlight the URL starting from the question mark onward, right-click, and Copy.
3. **Add the Tracking Link to the Message Template** — open your message template (text or email), paste the copied link by highlighting the desired text and selecting the Hyperlink option, then paste it into the hyperlink field. Save and send.
4. **Confirm Tracking** — before sending, ensure the tracking link is highlighted from the question mark onward; when the candidate applies via this link, the application is tracked specifically for you.

### Application Review & English Screening

1. Go to Application Review and add the following filters:
   - AWS video is not empty.
   - English Level at Native/Advanced level.
2. After you have a shortlist, click **Review X Applicants** (green button, top right).
3. Watch each candidate's video (the "M" button, top right). If you see an error, go back to the candidate profile, check the Summary tab under Social Links for a URL that starts with "hiring video."

**Check for English level in the video:** English is a non-negotiable requirement for most (if not all) of our clients. If the candidate's communication does not meet our criteria, reject them immediately using the **"Does Not Meet English Criteria"** rejection reason. **Do not send a rejection email** for this reason.

**Criteria to detect poor English in a video screening:**

- **Pronunciation & Clarity:** speech is hard to understand due to pronunciation issues; mispronounces common words; mumbles or speaks too softly to follow.
- **Fluency & Flow:** long pauses, frequent "uh/um/you know" fillers; struggles to form full sentences without hesitation; cannot maintain a natural pace (too slow, or rushed and unclear).
- **Grammar While Speaking:** incorrect verb tenses ("I go yesterday" instead of "I went"); broken sentence structure ("I no work before. I want job."); missing connectors ("I like marketing. Good. I work before.").

*(Note left in the source document: example videos illustrating these criteria were flagged to be added but are not yet included.)*

**Quality of video:** good lighting, clear audio, and a professional background are essential for a polished, professional appearance. If a candidate has great spoken English but bad video quality, send them the video-quality email template (see original Loom walkthrough for a screenshot of this) and check back the next day to see if they resent it.

**Skillset and compensation expectations match:**
1. **Resume Review:** look closely for keywords, experience, and how well it matches the job title. Watch for red flags like employment gaps, and double-check years of experience.
2. **Application Form Review:** carefully read how the candidate answered the qualifying questions to confirm they meet the job's needs.

**Advance Candidate:** if everything looks good, click "ADVANCE" to move them to the Tech Screen stage.

- Candidates with bad English who wouldn't fit any other role: **Archive.**
- Candidates with great English who don't fit this role but might fit another, and you want to keep them: click **Transfer**, select the bucket where they fit, and set them to the **Considered** stage. If no bucket fits, send them to the **Miscellaneous** bucket of your pod.

App Review should always be at 0, or as close to 0 as possible — pipelines need to stay clean.

| POD | First 24, max 48 hours | Day 0 – Day 5 | Day 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth and Brand | Send Batch 0 | First Shortlist of pre-vetted candidates: **9** | Send TRS |
| Rev Drivers | Send Batch 0 | First Shortlist of pre-vetted candidates: **12** | Send TRS |
| Tech & Product Builders | Send Batch 0 | First Shortlist of pre-vetted candidates: **8** | Send TRS |

(See also [[sla-recruitment-process|SLA Recruitment Process]] for how these day-ranges map onto the wider recruitment timeline.)

## Step 7: Recruiter Tech Screen Review

- Recruiter reviews Tech Screen candidates.
- **If strong:** pick the top 5 (min 3, max 7) and push them to the External Sheet stage; add them to the [[glossary-recruitment-delivery|TRS]] (Talent Review Sheet).
- **If not strong:** go to your sourcer and let them know the quality isn't meeting the mark — be clear and specific about what's missing or off, then ask them to send additional candidates.

## Step 8: End of Sourcer's Role (per Deal)

Assuming candidates move forward and get hired (or the deal reaches Closed Lost/Closed Won), the Sourcer's job on that deal ends here.

# Part B: Recruiter Workflow

This SOP outlines the step-by-step responsibilities for recruiters, from job kickoff through to final handoff to HR after a client selects a candidate.

## Step 1: Job Post Review (with Sourcer)

- Review the job post in Ashby alongside the sourcer once it's created.
- Confirm role expectations are clear and all relevant questions and info are included.

## Step 2: Batch 0 Review

- Review Batch 0 submitted by the sourcer.
- Wait for feedback from Batch 0 before sending new candidates.

## Step 3: AM Client Feedback

- The AM tags the recruiter and sourcer when there is a response from the client about Batch 0. The AM should cc the sourcer and Sourcing Coordinator in that email or Slack thread.

**If the client likes a candidate from Batch 0:**
- The Sourcer reaches out to the candidate along these lines: *"Hey, we have you as a candidate for this role because you are in our ATS. THIS is the job. The client already saw your profile and loved you. Now we need you to confirm you want to be considered. HERE is the JD. Please apply now in the next 4 hours and also please book a Disco call with our recruiter here."* After the application is sent, the Recruiter has the Disco call.
- Review candidates in the Tech Screen stage and select the top profiles to move to the TRS (External Sheet) stage.

**If the client doesn't approve any candidate from Batch 0:**
- Sourcer, AM, and recruiter (less so) should know and understand why the client said no — what the rejection reasons were, and how to identify a good profile for this client.
- The sourcer might need to go back to the job post (JD and app form) and change anything needed to match client expectations.
- Move forward with the provided feedback and shortlist candidates for Tech Screen for the recruiter.
- If it's worth it, send a new Batch 0 (Batch 0 v2) — for example, on a rescope due to wrong gender or wrong region.

## Step 6: Discovery Call Booking

Disco Calls are short, 15-minute screening calls held with candidates after the client has left feedback on the TRS and identified who they'd like to meet. They help confirm alignment with the client's needs, prepare candidates for upcoming interviews, and gather insights to fine-tune sourcing and screening. (See also [[candidate-screening-sop|Candidate Screening SOP]] and the [[glossary-recruitment-delivery|Disco]] glossary entry.)

**When to schedule:**
- Only after client feedback is received (TRS, Slack client channel, or email). If no feedback is visible, flag this to the AM during your DSU.
- 1–2 days before the Interview Block (never the same day).
- Try to hold Disco Calls within 2 days of receiving client feedback.

**Why this matters:** Disco Calls too close to the interview block leave candidates unprepared. Always give a buffer for reviewing prep materials, scheduling availability, and handling take-homes (if applicable).

**Before scheduling a Disco Call (in Ashby):**
- Click the role > Interview Plan tab > find the Disco Call stage and set the duration to 15 minutes.
- Under Slot #1: remove all existing team members, add yourself under Employees.
- Go to Meeting Hours and set your availability (Monday–Friday, your correct schedule).
- Under Direct Booking Link & Candidate Availability: adjust settings as needed and activate the Direct Booking Link.

**How to schedule a Disco Call:**
- Confirm you are the only recruiter assigned to the job.
- In Ashby: Jobs > search for the role > click it > Active candidates > move client-selected candidates to the Disco Call stage.
- Click the candidate > Schedule > Send Direct Booking Link > Email Direct Booking Link.
- Double-check the link works, then send it using the built-in email template.
- Check your Ashby availability to prevent overbooking or off-hours scheduling; adjust your calendar window if needed (e.g., limit to 2 days to maintain control).

**Prepping the candidate** (once they book):
- Send a prep email with the Job Description link (Job > Job Posting > View Public Posting > Copy Link), the client's website (from the New Search Form on Slack), and the Pre-Interview Non-Circumvention Agreement (must be signed before the interview — ask the candidate before starting).
- In the candidate's Ashby profile: click the email icon, start a new email draft, select the Prep template (search "prep"), choose the second option, insert links, and send.
- Email should include: a brief intro and congratulations, the job description, the client's website, the Non-Circumvention Agreement, and what to expect in the Disco Call.

**During the Disco Call:**
- Be punctual — never late.
- Have your script and checklist ready.
- Camera on, look presentable.
- Record the call and enable transcripts.
- Ask the candidate if they signed the Non-Circumvention Agreement (if not, have them sign on the call).
- Use the TRS "Submit Feedback" link to add notes directly to the candidate's section.

**What to cover:** confirm their background aligns with the role; verify what they wrote in their application; confirm salary expectations; notice period. Prioritize relevant questions — the call is only 15 minutes.

**Providing feedback/suggestions:** before closing, if you spot improvement areas, share constructive suggestions after outlining next steps (e.g., mentioning specific achievements tied to KPIs, campaign results for marketing roles, social media metrics for social roles).

**End the call by:** confirming next steps, reminding them of their upcoming Take-Home (if any), and letting them know when to expect the next update.

**If a candidate performs poorly in the Discovery Call:**
- Immediately notify the AM that the candidate is not a good fit and will not be moving forward.
- Proactively source a replacement — revisit candidates from your technical screening pool who showed potential, or coordinate with your sourcer to prioritize finding a stronger candidate as soon as possible.

**After the call:**
- Update the TRS "Scale Army Team Status" block with the Disco Call stage, so everyone knows the candidate's exact position in the process.
- Save your call notes in the designated section.
- Watch the SOP videos for: how to take proper notes, an example of good notes, and how to reset call availability in Ashby.

## Step 7: Schedule Client Interview Block

If the candidate performs well in the Discovery Call, align with the AM in your pod Slack channel on a block of time for interviews. Before the client interview, an Audio & Video prep call with the candidate must be scheduled — see [[av-prep-calls-for-client-interview|A/V Prep Calls for Client Interview]] and [[candidate-interview-process|Candidate Interview Process]] for the fuller written procedure and recorded walkthrough (this SOP gives the sourcing/recruiting-specific detail below).

**Scheduling candidates into interview blocks:**
- If you approve the candidate after the Disco call: confirm their availability, schedule them in the Interview Block, and ensure the A/V Prep Call and Interview Block use the same link.
- If no Interview Block exists yet: once you finish Disco calls for all candidates the client wants to meet, ask the AM to schedule a block. When the AM schedules it, make sure the calendar event does *not* include a video link (if it does, ask the AM to remove it). After scheduling, clean up your ClickUp task and keep all information clear and organized.

**Sending interview prep materials to candidates:**
- The AM sends you a Loom video explaining the client and interview structure, plus a written prep document — 1–2 days before the interview.
- Email prep materials to candidates at least 1 day before the interview, including the Loom video, the prep document, and date/time confirmation. Use the "Interview Reminder – Candidate (must be customized)" email template, adding date/time manually.
- Important reminders: Discovery calls must be completed 1–2 days before the interview block; if the block date needs to move, inform the AM on Slack (#scale) or in the DSU meeting; ask for edit access to the AM's calendar to help with coordination.

**A/V Prep Calls & Client Interviews:**

A/V (Audio/Visual) Prep Calls are held 5 minutes before a candidate's interview to test internet, camera, and microphone quality, ensure the candidate is in a quiet well-lit space, and offer moral support and last-minute reminders.

Checklist for A/V Prep Calls: confirm stable internet and working audio/video; confirm a professional setting; provide encouragement and remind the candidate to stay calm and confident; obtain the candidate's consent to record before the client joins, then start recording.

Once the client joins: the AM introduces the candidate & recruiter; the recruiter exits the call after the introduction; the AM ends the recording.

After the interview, the AM is responsible for gathering and posting client feedback in **#scale**, and clarifying next steps (second interview, pass, offer). The recruiter's post-interview responsibilities: read and react to the feedback, implement action items (e.g., change search criteria, prep improvements), and ask questions if feedback is unclear.

## Step 8: Take-Home Assignment (If Applicable)

A Take-Home Assignment lets the client evaluate a candidate's hands-on experience, problem-solving ability, and/or technical skills — typically follows a successful first-round interview.

**How we get it:** if the client wants to move the candidate forward, the AM requests an assignment from the client, or builds it for them if the client doesn't have one.

**Review the Take-Home Assignment:**
- Check delivery format — link, email, screenshot, or text. Save it in the correct job folder or shared drive.
- Open and review in full: instructions, tasks, and deliverables should all make sense and be suitable for the candidate. If anything is unclear, missing, or seems incorrect, ask the AM for clarification before proceeding.

**Set up the assignment in Ashby:**
- Go to the job > Interview Plan > scroll to the Take-Home Assignment stage.
- Click the default assignment to edit it; add yourself (or the appropriate reviewer) as the reviewer.
- Customize the fields:
  - **Client Name:** replace any placeholder.
  - **Role Title:** update with the correct role (e.g., Marketing Generalist).
  - **File Naming:** update instructions for how candidates should name their file (e.g., "Name your PDF as: YourName – RoleTitle – ClientName").
  - **Due Date:** enter the exact due date and time from the AM/client. Always specify the time zone.
  - **Instructions:** paste in the assignment content; double-check for typos, and edit for clarity, grammar, and formatting.

**Format for clarity:** remove unnecessary headers (e.g., "Header 3"); keep spacing/bolding/underlining/bullets consistent; separate parts clearly ("Part One," "Part Two"); review the whole assignment as if you were the candidate.

**Save and preview:** save all edits in Ashby, preview how it will appear to the candidate, and confirm names, deadlines, instructions, and file naming are accurate. See the [Loom on creating the Take-Home Assignment in Ashby](https://www.loom.com/share/e14c3d31203842cd83bda75a6033996d?sid=4550e9a4-d158-4622-ab30-5c845f8beddb).

**Move candidate to Take-Home stage and send:** move the candidate in Ashby from their current stage (e.g., "Interview with HM") to "Take-Home Assignment" — this triggers the take-home workflow, letting the candidate submit directly through the platform.

**Use the standard Ashby email template** with the "assessment" keyword to send the take-home; confirm it includes the assignment link/instructions, the file naming format, the submission deadline and time zone, and contact info for questions.

**Example candidate email:**

> **Subject:** Next Step – Take-Home Assignment for {Role Title}
>
> Hey there! If you're reading this, you've made it to the next stage of our hiring process — congratulations!
>
> Below is your Take-Home Assignment prepared by the [{Client Name}](Insert Client Website) team to help us understand your approach and skills.
>
> **The Assignment:** (Insert link or instructions here)
>
> **Deliverables:**
> - Submit your assignment as a PDF
> - Include your full name, role title, and client name at the top of the document
> - File name format: YourName – RoleTitle – ClientName
>
> **Submission Deadline:** (Insert due date and time, with time zone)
>
> Good luck, and let us know if you have any questions!

See the [Loom on sending a take-home assignment email](https://www.loom.com/share/a4a0f98b041f4ac0ae842f790fbfcdfc?sid=fc53f6db-4baf-42fc-8113-f1af6cdb728f).

**Take-Home submission & review:** once submitted, check grammar & structure, accuracy (all parts completed), and correct file naming. For technical roles, request support from **Liz (Senior Recruiter)** or **Uday (Talent Acquisition Manager)** to help evaluate submissions if needed.

**Finalizing the submission:** once reviewed and approved, download the final submission, upload it to the candidate's App Packet in the TRS, and add a new column named "Take Home Assignment" next to the qualifying questions, linking the assignment there.

**Sharing with the team:** post the Take-Home link in the **#IRS-Review** Slack channel thread for the role, tagging the responsible Account Manager.

## Step 9: Candidate Offer & Handoff to HR

Once the client says yes to a candidate:
- Inform HR.
- Book the candidate on an Opportunity Call using the proper calendar.

At this point, HR takes over and continues the process from offer to onboarding. See [[recruiter-offer-stage-how-to-give-an-offer|Recruiter: Offer Stage — How to Give Out an Offer]] and [[sla-recruitment-process|SLA Recruitment Process]] for how the offer/onboarding steps continue from here.

## Step 10: Close the Role in Ashby

After the client has signed the MSA and the talent has started:

**Recruiter:**
1. Candidates who are good but were not hired (i.e., could work for another role):
   - Archive and reject them with the template that says we will consider them for other roles.
   - All candidates who reached at least the External Sheet stage: consider them for the role-specific generic bucket, into the Tech Screen stage.
   - All candidates who reached anything before Tech Screen: (no further action specified in the source beyond archiving as above).

**Sourcer:**
2. Make sure the pipeline is clean — no one left in any active stage. All candidates should be Archived or Considered/Transferred.

**Then:**
3. Once the pipeline is clean, select **Closed Won** or **Closed Lost**.

## Checklist

**Sourcing:**
- [ ] Confirm the new search is assigned (POD, AM, Recruiter, Sourcer) via #new-searches.
- [ ] Create the job in Ashby from the External Search Master Template; fill POD, hiring team, and location(s) (max 1 LATAM country for Automatic Slots, max 3 African countries; never post to a banned country).
- [ ] Build the application form as a copy of the Master Application Form Template (8 questions, years-of-experience question first).
- [ ] Set up automation, publish the job, and reply in #new-searches with the links.
- [ ] Sourcer + recruiter + Sourcing Coordinator (Youssef) all QA the post.
- [ ] Build and send Batch 0 within 24–48 hours (see [[batch-zero-process|Batch Zero Process]]).
- [ ] Filter Application Review by AWS video present + English level; reject poor-English videos immediately with no rejection email; advance/transfer/archive per criteria; keep App Review at ~0.
- [ ] Hit sourcing quotas per POD by Day 5; push top candidates to Tech Screen.
- [ ] Use custom tracking links for every outbound/LinkedIn application channel.

**Recruiting:**
- [ ] Review the job post and Batch 0 with the sourcer.
- [ ] On client feedback, move approved candidates to Client Said Yes; on rejection, gather rejection reasons and coordinate a Batch 0 v2 if warranted.
- [ ] Build and send the TRS workbook to the AM.
- [ ] Schedule Disco Calls 1–2 days before the Interview Block, only after client feedback; prep candidates and record the call.
- [ ] Schedule the Interview Block and A/V Prep Call on the same link; send prep materials at least 1 day ahead.
- [ ] Send Take-Home Assignments (if applicable), review submissions, and share with the team via #IRS-Review.
- [ ] On client yes: inform HR and book the Opportunity Call.
- [ ] On close (Closed Won/Lost): archive/transfer all remaining candidates and clean the pipeline before marking the role closed.
