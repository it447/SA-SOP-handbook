## Goal

A clear, step-by-step guide for the recruitment process, ensuring consistency, clarity, and efficiency from deal creation to deal closure.

## Who this is for

Sales, Client Success (CS), Account Managers (AMs), Recruiters, Recruiting Leadership, and Recruiting Ops.

## Stage 1: Deal Creation & Approval

**Step 1 — Deal Creation (Sales / CS):**
- Sales or CS creates the deal in HubSpot.
- The deal is posted in the JD Approval Channel.
- Recruiting leadership reviews the JD and raises concerns or requests changes if needed.
- Once approved, the search moves forward.

**Step 2 — Deposit & Search Authorization:**
- Client pays the required deposit.
- Once the deposit is received, the search is officially authorized and launched.

## Stage 2: Team Assignment & Internal Setup

**Step 3 — Pod Assignment:**
- Role is shared in the Pod Assignment Channel.
- Portfolio & AM assignment is determined by Zeina.
- Recruiter assignment is determined by Recruiting Leadership.

**Step 4 — Internal Communication:**
- The search is announced in the New Searches Channel; all stakeholders get visibility, and the assigned recruiter is auto-tagged.

**Step 5 — Search Creation (Recruiting Ops):**
- Recruiting Ops creates the role in Ashby, builds/configures the Application Form, and develops screening questions based on client requirements. Recruiting Ops shares the new role with the assigned recruiter for review.

## Stage 3: Search Launch & Client Alignment

**Step 6 — Batch 0 Submission:**
- The recruiting team sends Batch 0 candidates within 1–2 business days after search launch.

**Step 7 — Kickoff Call (KOC):**
- The AM schedules and leads the kickoff call with the client, then documents and shares notes, expectations, and clarifications with the recruiter.

## Stage 4: Candidate Pipeline Development

**Step 8 — First Batch Submission:**
- Recruiters source and screen candidates, then submit the First Candidate Batch — typically 5–8 business days after the kickoff call.

## Stage 5: Candidate Evaluation & Preparation

**Step 9 — Client Feedback & Candidate Confirmation:**
- Client provides feedback on submitted candidates. For shortlisted candidates, the recruiter schedules a 15-minute [[glossary-recruitment-delivery|Discovery Call]] and reconfirms salary expectations, relevant experience, and interest/availability.

**Step 9B — Candidate Preparation:**
- The AM prepares prep material (client overview, company info, role details, interview expectations) and the recruiter shares it with candidates.

## Stage 6: Interview Process

**Step 10 — Interview Block:**
- Typically occurs 1–2 days after candidate preparation. AM and recruiter join the call with the candidate 5 minutes before the interview; the recruiter confirms candidate readiness and leaves; the AM and candidate then join the client interview.

**Step 11 — Additional Interviews & Assessment:**
- Standard process includes a minimum of 2 interview rounds, plus a Take-Home Assessment (THA) or case study.

## Stage 7: Offer & Closing

**Step 12 — Final Decision:** After interviews and THA review, the client selects the candidate.

**Step 13 — LOI (AM Responsibility):** The AM sends the Letter of Intent once the start date is known.

**Step 14 — Offer Call:** Recruiter + HR conduct the Job Offer Call with the candidate, reviewing compensation, benefits, and logistics.

**Step 15 — Offer & Contract:** HR sends the Offer Letter and Employment Contract; the candidate signs.

**Step 16 — MSA & Deal Closure:** The AM sends the MSA to the client; the role is officially Closed Won once signed; Recruiting Ops closes the deal in Ashby.

## General Timeline Overview

- Deposit → Search Launch → 1–2 Days → Batch 0
- Kickoff Call → 5–8 Business Days → First Batch
- Interview Process → Assessment → Offer → MSA → Closed Won
