## Goal

Ensure candidates are fully prepared for the client interview by verifying their technical setup — audio and video quality.

## Who this is for

Recruiters, Account Managers (AMs).

## Note

The primary reference for this SOP is a recorded video walkthrough rather than a static doc. Recruiters run the A/V Prep Call 5 minutes before each interview, confirming internet, lighting, and audio (see the [[am-sop-bible|AM SOP Bible]], Step Four, for how this fits into the Interview Block).
