## Goal

Answer a handful of recurring questions about Ashby roles/terminology, candidate stage movement, and rejection process that weren't covered elsewhere in the recruitment handbook, so new team members (and the chatbot) have a clear, sourced answer instead of guessing.

## Who this is for

New recruiters, Recruiting Coordinators, and anyone unclear on Ashby role names or the mechanics of moving a candidate through Interviewing/Offer/rejection.

## How do we onboard a new recruiter (or anyone) into Ashby?

There is no written click-by-click onboarding SOP — new team members are taught hands-on through **shadowing with real examples**. A senior team member shares their screen and walks the new hire through each step of the live process, always following the [recruiting process SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FcNtTOHQjr3WNRSOfDy36zL_fPg90viK6jL9RqjcvW4/edit?usp=sharing) (Google Doc).

- **Recruiters** are taught how to use Ashby to search the candidate database and move candidates through the pipeline — from **New Leads** and **Application Review** through **Job Offer** — plus how to send emails using the various templates, and how to send candidates for review.
- **Recruiting Coordinators** are taught how to open, post, edit, and close jobs in Ashby.

## What's the difference between "Recruiter" and "Recruiting Coordinator" in Ashby?

Ashby's built-in role names don't line up one-to-one with Scale Army's actual titles — there is no "Account Manager" role in Ashby at all. The mapping is:

| Ashby role | Scale Army title |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Account Manager (AM) |
| Recruiting Coordinator | Recruiter |

See the [[glossary-recruitment-delivery|Recruitment & Delivery Glossary]] for the AM/Recruiter/Recruiting Coordinator definitions. Keep this in mind whenever a job's "hiring team" assignment in Ashby is discussed — "Recruiter" in that context means the AM, not the person Scale Army calls a Recruiter.

## How do we move a candidate from "Interviewing" to "Offer" in Ashby?

Select the candidate and drag them to the **Offer** stage.

**Correction (per Youssef Singer, Sourcing Coordinator):** whether this triggers an automatic email depends on how that interview stage is configured — Ashby *can* send an automatic email on a stage move if one is set up under that stage's email/interview settings. Check the specific job's Interview Plan configuration for the Offer stage rather than assuming either way.

If no automatic email is configured for that stage, the team has to manually:
1. Email the candidate to let them know the client is interested in moving forward.
2. Schedule a call to make the offer.

See [[recruiter-offer-stage-how-to-give-an-offer|Recruiter: Offer Stage — How to Give Out an Offer]] for the fuller offer-call procedure once this stage move happens.

## What's the process for rejecting a candidate after a final-round interview?

Send a personalized email thanking them for participating in the process, including as much specific feedback as possible. This is intentionally a **manual step** — it is not templated/automated — precisely so the message stays personal after a candidate has gone through a full interview process.

- [Ashby email template link](https://app.ashbyhq.com/admin/organizational-settings/email-templates/897dd54d-252b-4957-88ea-c23edba456ad) (starting point, not a copy-paste-as-is template).
- [Additional guidance on how and what to communicate to candidates](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FcNtTOHQjr3WNRSOfDy36zL_fPg90viK6jL9RqjcvW4/edit?tab=t.g601efo8ily0#heading=h.ttgm6rg1rny6) (same recruiting process doc, specific section).

## What's the SOP for sourcing candidates on LinkedIn?

**There is no written SOP for LinkedIn sourcing itself.** Sourcing is treated as a skill that develops through practice, not a checklist — junior recruiters are trained via official LinkedIn training recordings plus shadowing/hands-on practice with real examples:

- [LinkedIn Hiring Assistant pre-screening](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7488409)
- [Best practices for candidate sourcing](https://www.linkedin.com/smart-links/AQHI_nZ4uZqZ9w)

What **is** documented is the mechanical side of posting/managing a role on LinkedIn and re-engaging apply-starters — see [[recruiting-ops-sop|Recruiting Ops SOP]]'s "LinkedIn: Post, Open, Refresh, and Close" and "InMails to Apply Starters" sections for those step-by-step procedures; this FAQ doesn't repeat them.

## Checklist

- [ ] Onboarding a new recruiter/coordinator: pair them with a senior teammate for hands-on shadowing against the recruiting process SOP — there's no self-serve doc to hand them instead.
- [ ] Remember the Ashby role mapping (Recruiter = AM, Recruiting Coordinator = Recruiter) before assigning or reading a job's hiring team in Ashby.
- [ ] Moving a candidate to Offer: manually email the candidate and schedule the offer call yourself — Ashby won't notify anyone automatically.
- [ ] Rejecting after a final-round interview: always send a personalized, feedback-rich email — don't rely on an automated template for this stage.
- [ ] For LinkedIn sourcing skill-building, point new sourcers to the training recordings and set up shadowing rather than looking for a written SOP that doesn't exist.
- [ ] For LinkedIn job posting/InMail mechanics, use [[recruiting-ops-sop|Recruiting Ops SOP]] rather than this doc.
