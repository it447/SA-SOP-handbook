## Goal

Standardize the process of screening candidates, ensuring efficient and effective evaluation through interviews, feedback, and take-home tasks.

## Who this is for

Recruiters and Account Managers (AMs).

## Steps

**1. [[glossary-recruitment-delivery|Disco Call]] (Recruiter + Candidate interview call):**
- Introduce yourself and explain Scale Army.
- Ask the candidate to introduce themselves.
- Send the client's website for role/company familiarity.
- Ask role-specific questions.
- Confirm salary expectations and EST working hours.
- Run an internet speed test if time allows.
- Explain next steps.

**2. Take-Home Assignment:**
- Send the take-home assignment to the candidate after the Disco call.
- Provide clear submission instructions (PDF with name, role, and client noted).

**3. Job Offer Discussion (Offer):**
- Confirm start date and salary details with the candidate.
- Send the offer letter for review and signature.
- Onboard the candidate on Deel once signed.
- Send the 1:1 onboarding template and "How to Keep Your Job" slides.
- Check for updates on the 1:1 document.

**4. Creating Questionnaires:**
- Create or amend questionnaires in Ashby.
- Automate questionnaires based on job title and interview stage.
