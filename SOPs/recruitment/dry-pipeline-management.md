## Goal

Track and manage openings with a dry pipeline — making sure the LinkedIn job posting is live, has the right title, accurate compensation, and is targeting the right audience. Recruiting Ops and Supply Marketing hold a daily call before the daily Recruiting Calls to review the dry pipeline list.

## Who this is for

Recruitment team.

The working list for this SOP lives in a live spreadsheet rather than a static procedure doc.
