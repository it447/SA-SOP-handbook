- **EOR (Employer of Record):** A service where Scale Army manages payroll and holds compliance for talent on behalf of the client, without being the talent's actual employer.
- **Buyout:** The process of buying talent out of their contract with Scale Army, transitioning them to a direct placement with the client.
- **Direct Placement:** When the client hires the talent directly, bypassing Scale Army's recruitment process post-placement.
- **NTC (Net to Candidate):** The salary paid directly to the candidate after Scale Army's margin is deducted.
- **GM (Gross Margin):** Scale Army's margin on a talent placement, calculated from the total amount the client pays.
- **CW (Closed Won):** A deal where Scale Army successfully placed a candidate with the client.
- **CL (Closed Lost):** A deal where Scale Army was unable to place a candidate with the client.
- **Onboarding Call:** A call where Scale Army educates the client on best practices for managing remote talent, Scale Army processes, and expectations for a smooth collaboration.
