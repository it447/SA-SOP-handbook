## Goal

To outline the process for managing and assigning new searches in Slack.

## Who this is for

Account Managers (AMs)

## Process

**New Search Alert** — when a new search comes in, it is posted with the role, pod, company, and NTC (all-in) range. Example:

- Example Role: Tracking & Front-End Engineer
- Example Pod: Product & Tech
- Example Company: Trim RX
- NTC - ALL IN: 3k – 6.7K

The alert message asks the Pod's Account Manager and Recruiter to [[recruitment-process-step-by-step|confirm ownership]], e.g.: "@Zeina, @Fer / @Liz, can you please confirm: Who is the Account Manager for this pod? (Insert name) scoped this and is in frequent touch with the client. Who is the Recruiter?"
