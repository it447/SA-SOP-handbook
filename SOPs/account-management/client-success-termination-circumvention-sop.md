## Goal

Explain what to do when a client wants to end the contract and there's a concern they may be circumventing Scale Army (going direct with placed talent without paying the appropriate fees).

## Who this is for

Account Managers, Finance, and Client Success.

## Checklist

1. Client Success checks the MSA for the client's required notice period.
2. Client Success informs the client of the required 30 days' notice, if applicable.
3. Client Success works with HR to investigate whether circumvention is happening.
4. HR gets on a call with the talent to check for signs of circumvention, documenting anything suspicious.
5. If the client owes money and circumvention is suspected, Client Success tells the Head of Sales.
6. The Head of Sales works with Alex to have Lisa send a Demand Letter.
7. The Demand Letter includes: any fees owed from the 30-day notice period, and any fees owed for circumvention.
