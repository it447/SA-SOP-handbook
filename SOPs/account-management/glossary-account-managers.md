---
title: "Glossary for Account Managers (AMs)"
department: account-management
category: glossary
last_updated: 2026-07-20
---

- **Kick-Off Call:** The initial meeting with a new client, where the AM introduces themselves, sets expectations, clarifies role requirements, and discusses next steps. Builds rapport and the foundation for the partnership.
- **IRS (Internal Review Sheet):** A recruiter-created document with detailed candidate information, used by the AM to assess candidate suitability before the CRS. This is where the AM adds margin to calculate pricing/markup.
- **TRS (Talent Review Sheet):** The client-facing version of the IRS — a curated candidate list with resumes, interview notes, and feedback, sent ahead of the CRS, without margin and with all-in cost shown per candidate.
- **CRS (Candidate Review Session):** A meeting between AM and client where the client reviews TRS candidates; the AM facilitates discussion, collects feedback, and schedules interviews for top candidates.
- **Onboarding Call:** Held after the client confirms they want to move forward with a candidate — covers work arrangements, start date, and final details. Run by Client Success; marks the handoff point from AM to CS.
- **Interview Block:** A scheduled block where the client interviews multiple candidates. The AM facilitates scheduling and logistics; recruiters add candidates and finalize the block.
- **Candidate Presentation Block:** A short meeting where the candidate walks the client through their take-home task/project and answers questions.
- **Offer Call:** Held after the client decides to move forward with a candidate — the recruiter confirms salary, start date, and other offer terms.
- **MSA (Master Services Agreement):** The formal contract between Scale Army and the client outlining terms of the hiring process, signed once the client agrees to proceed.
- **Addendum:** A document adding or changing specific terms of an existing MSA, typically for existing clients adjusting scope on a new search.
- **LOI (Letter of Intent):** Sent once a candidate is selected, outlining agreed-upon terms (start date, salary) for the client's review/signature before the formal offer.
