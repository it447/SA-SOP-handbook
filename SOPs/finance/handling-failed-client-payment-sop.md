## Goal

Ensure prompt action when a client payment fails — minimizing disruption to talent services while providing a clear escalation path if payment isn't resolved.

## Who this is for

Finance Team (owner), with Client Success Team and HR Team support.

## Systems used

Stripe (invoice/payment processing), Slack (internal coordination), Email (client communication), Deel (talent payroll), All Sales Database (talent status verification).

## Process overview

Stripe processes recurring payments based on the talent's start date; ACH payments begin processing 5 days before the recurring billing date. If a payment fails, Finance notifies the client immediately and starts a 3-day grace period. If unresolved, Finance may attempt to charge the payment method on file per the MSA. If that also fails, the talent engagement is suspended, payroll is paused, and the issue escalates internally.

## Step-by-step process

**1. Identify the failed payment** — Finance monitors Stripe. If payment fails or isn't completed by the billing date, the process starts immediately.

**2. Day 0 — Immediate client notification:** Finance emails the client (cc: client finance contact if available, the Client Success Manager, and HR), explaining the payment failed, providing a payment link, and noting that the talent will need to pause work if payment isn't received within 3 business days.

**3. Day 1 — Finance follow-up:** After 24 hours, Finance checks Stripe for any payment attempt. If none, the Finance Associate contacts the client directly (phone/message/email), and the CSM confirms the client received the invoice and helps resolve any blockers.

**4. Day 2 — Final reminder:** If still unpaid, Finance sends a final-notice email (same recipient list) warning that the talent will need to stop working if payment isn't received by end of day tomorrow.

**5. Attempt to charge the payment method on file:** If the client remains unresponsive, Finance may charge the stored payment method as permitted by the MSA. If successful, the process ends and the talent continues normally. If it fails, move to suspension.

**6. Day 3 — Suspend talent services:** If unresolved after the 3-day grace period, Finance (with Client Success) sends a suspension notice to the client, client finance team, CSM, HR, and the talent, pausing the talent's services effective immediately, resumable once the invoice is settled. The message to the talent is determined by HR.

**7. Pause payroll:** Finance notifies HR to pause payroll for the affected talent in Deel. The contract stays active — only payroll payments pause, resuming once payment is received.

**8. Escalate internally if still unresolved:** Finance notifies the Senior Operations Manager, who reviews the case and escalates to the CEO if necessary. Further actions may include late payment fees (if specified in the MSA), contract termination, legal escalation, or collections.

## Payment resolution

Once payment is received: Finance confirms it in Stripe, HR resumes payroll in Deel, Client Success tells the talent to resume work, and billing continues on the normal schedule.

## Documentation & reporting

Finance keeps records of the full process — failed payment identification, client communications, charge attempts, suspension notices, payroll pause notifications, and escalation actions — to support audit compliance, revenue tracking, and operational visibility.
