## Goal

Verify all salary-related information — base pay, commissions, bonuses, reimbursements, deductions — before payroll is finalized, cross-checking HR-prepared data against Slack communications and the All Sales Database.

## Who this is for

Finance Team, HR Team, and Payroll Administrators. Process owner: Finance & Operations Associate, with HR supporting payroll prep.

## Systems used

Slack (`#payroll-prep`, `#hr-billing-requests`, `#offboarding-lost-deals`), HR Payroll Prep Sheets (external + internal), All Sales Database, Stripe (client charges when applicable), Email, and the Finance Comparison Sheet (reconciliation).

## Step-by-step process

**1. Receive payroll prep from HR** — sheets covering base salary, adjustments, bonuses, commissions, reimbursements, deductions, and other compensation info, for both external talent and internal contractors.

**2. Review compensation adjustments** — check bonuses, commissions, reimbursements, salary adjustments, and deductions, confirming each is backed by internal records or communication.

**3. Cross-check internal communications** — review the relevant Slack channels (HR compensation updates, bonus/commission notifications, reimbursement approvals, offboarding updates) to confirm every adjustment in the prep sheets matches an approved communication.

**4. Verify talent status in the All Sales Database** — confirm whether the talent is active, recently offboarded, or in transition during the payroll period. If offboarded mid-month, confirm whether pending compensation still needs to be included.

**5. Reconcile using the Finance Comparison Sheet** — cross-reference HR payroll prep sheets, Slack communications, and All Sales Database records to ensure consistency across sources.

**6. Verify client billing impact** — for external talent, determine whether an adjustment (commission, bonus, reimbursement) should be billed to the client per the MSA, and update Stripe accordingly. If the company absorbs the cost (e.g. internal stipends), make no client billing changes.

**7. Confirm client payment status** — before applying billing adjustments in Stripe, verify the client's account is current and prior invoices are on schedule.

**8. Final payroll verification** — confirm payroll data reflects all adjustments accurately, any client billing is properly recorded, and records are consistent across systems before payroll proceeds.

## Data sources

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Payroll preparation | HR Payroll Prep Sheets |
| Compensation adjustments | Slack communications |
| Talent operational status | All Sales Database |
| Client billing adjustments | Stripe |
| Internal verification | Finance Comparison Sheet |

## Notes

- Check all relevant Slack channels and data points before finalizing payroll.
- Any client charges must align with the MSA.
- HR leads initial payroll prep; Finance owns final validation.
