## Goal

An overview of the Finance and Operations department, its core responsibilities, and how it supports other departments.

## Who this is for

Finance Team, Operations Team, and Leadership.

## Department purpose

Finance & Operations is responsible for financial health, operational efficiency, and data visibility company-wide — executing financial transactions, billing, payroll, and reporting accurately and consistently, and giving leadership reliable data for decision-making. It works cross-functionally with Sales, Marketing, Client Success, Recruitment, and HR.

## Core responsibilities

- Invoice creation and management
- Client billing and revenue tracking
- Payroll and contractor payments
- Financial reporting and forecasting
- Financial data tracking and validation
- Cross-department operational coordination
- Financial process documentation and system maintenance

## Department structure

Four roles cover governance, analysis/process design, operational execution, and documentation/admin support.

**Senior Finance Manager** — leads the function; owns financial strategy, internal controls/governance, final sign-off authority, cross-functional leadership, KPI ownership, approval authority over transactions/budget commitments, risk management, and leadership reporting. Sets direction rather than executing day-to-day admin work.

**Senior Finance Associate** (Finance Analyst / Operational Finance Lead) — owns payroll calculations/validation/reconciliation, variable comp/bonus/commission calculations, budgets and forecasts, margin/revenue/cost/workforce analysis, KPI methodology, and finance process design/automation recommendations.

**Finance & Operations Associate** — the operational backbone: budget tracking/monitoring/variance reporting (final approval stays with the Senior Finance Manager), payroll execution/coordination (collecting inputs, coordinating timelines, submitting for processing — calculations/validation stay with the Senior Finance Associate), administering AP/AR/expense platforms/internal tools, processing client invoices and vendor payments, and managing subscription/software payments.

**Administrative Support Generalist** — enables the team through SOP documentation/version control, process mapping, KPI reporting support/dashboard maintenance, data validation, and meeting coordination. Does not make or approve financial decisions.

## Systems & tools

HubSpot (CRM data/revenue tracking), Ashby, Stripe, internal financial spreadsheets/trackers (including the company-wide sales database), shared Drive documentation, QuickBooks, and Ramp.

## Data ownership & cross-department reporting

Finance & Operations is the central point for financial/operational data across departments. Each department generates metrics that affect financial performance; Finance aggregates and validates them for reporting, forecasting, and decision-making. Connected departments: Marketing, Sales, Account Management, Recruitment, Client Success, and HR.
