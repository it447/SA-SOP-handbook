## Goal

A shared reference for financial, operational, and company-specific terminology, so terms are used consistently across teams and new hires can get up to speed quickly.

## Who this is for

Primarily Finance & Operations, but also Sales, HR, and anyone at Scale Army who needs to interpret financial, commission, or company-specific terms.

## Finance & accounting fundamentals

- **Gross Margin (GM):** Revenue minus the direct cost of delivering the service (mainly contractor pay) — usually expressed as a dollar amount per placement and the primary basis for commissions. E.g. a $3,000/month bill with $2,500 contractor pay yields a $500 GM.
- **Gross Margin Percentage (GM%):** GM ÷ Revenue. E.g. a 20% GM% on $3,000 billing means $600 retained before overhead.
- **EBITDA:** Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization — a profitability proxy used as a leadership-level variable comp target.
- **Net Income (NI):** Bottom-line profit after all expenses, interest, and taxes (distinct from EBITDA).
- **Revenue:** Total client billing before deductions — primarily Stripe recurring charges from active placements, plus one-time items (buyouts, deposits, fees).
- **Accounts Receivable (AR):** Money owed for services rendered but not yet collected — e.g. a $5,000 Stripe bill that hasn't cleared yet sits in AR.
- **P&L:** Profit and Loss statement — summarizes revenue, costs, and expenses over a period; the basis for budget-vs-actuals.
- **Budget vs. Actuals (BvA):** Comparing the financial plan to real results for the same period, using an 11+1 structure.
- **Forecasting:** Predicting future performance from current data/trends, implemented via the 11+1 Forecast.
- **11+1 Forecast:** A rolling forecast — 1 closed month of actuals + 11 months of budget = a full-year view; each month's budget figure is replaced by actuals as it closes.
- **Variance:** The difference between budgeted and actual figures (favorable or unfavorable), in dollars and percentage.
- **COGS:** Cost of Goods Sold — direct costs of producing revenue; here, mainly contractor comp and platform fees (Stripe, Deel).
- **OpEx:** Operating Expenses — indirect costs to run the business (internal salaries, software, advertising, overhead).
- **Passthrough Cost:** A cost billed to the client at exactly what the company paid, with no markup (e.g. a $39/contractor background check).
- **Contra-Revenue:** A deduction recorded as a negative revenue line item rather than an expense (e.g. refunds, service credits).
- **FX Policy:** Rules governing how exchange-rate fluctuations are handled for non-USD compensation or billing.

## Staffing & placement concepts

- **Placement:** The successful hiring of a candidate by a client — the core revenue event; most commissions trigger from placements.
- **IRS (Internal Review Sheet):** A recruiter-created candidate document used for internal assessment before client submission. (At Scale Army this acronym only refers to this document, not the U.S. tax agency.)
- **All Sales Database:** The central Finance-maintained sheet recording every placement (recruiter, AM, AE, client, start date, GM) — the single source of truth for commissions.
- **30-Day Retention Gate:** The placed contractor must stay active with the client 30 days before the first commission tranche pays.
- **90-Day Retention Gate:** Same concept, for the second commission tranche.
- **Clawback:** Recovering a commission already paid, e.g. after a placement is found to have been misattributed.
- **Churn:** The rate at which placed contractors leave a client; high churn raises clawback risk and lowers profitability.
- **Retention:** Keeping placed contractors active with clients and employees engaged over time.
- **Onboarding / Offboarding:** Integrating a new client/contractor into Scale Army's systems / formally closing out a placement when the engagement ends.
- **Batch 0:** An early-stage/pre-pipeline sourcing batch delivered before the standard pipeline (the "0" doesn't mean zero placements).
- **Deposit:** The upfront payment required to launch a search — a core AE commission trigger alongside the signed Engagement Letter.
- **Engagement Letter:** The signed agreement formalizing a recruiting/staffing engagement — a commission trigger for some roles.
- **MSA:** Master Service Agreement — the broader contract governing the client relationship; signing can also trigger commission.
- **EOR (Employer of Record):** A third party (Deel, for Scale Army) that formally employs contractors on a client's behalf, handling payroll/taxes/compliance.
- **BPO:** Business Process Outsourcing — clients who outsource an entire function/team rather than filling individual roles (often shorthand for specific named BPO clients internally); commission structures often differ.
- **Buyout:** A client pays to buy a placed talent out of their Scale Army contract to hire them directly.
- **Direct Placement:** The resulting arrangement after a Buyout — the client hires directly and billing through Scale Army ends.
- **PTO:** Paid time off for a placed contractor, managed through their EOR/engagement terms.

## Roles & organizational structure

- **AE (Account Executive):** Acquires new clients (inbound + outbound sales).
- **AM (Account Manager):** Manages ongoing client relationships and placements within existing accounts; earns commission on 30/90-day retention.
- **AM Lead:** Manages the AM team; earns team-level bonuses plus personal placement commissions.
- **POD Leader:** Leads a small "pod" of AMs or Recruiters; earns personal deal commissions plus team performance bonuses.
- **Recruiter:** Sources, screens, and places candidates.
- **Senior Recruiter / Team Lead (SR/TL):** An experienced recruiter who also manages a small team; earns personal commissions plus a small override on team placements.
- **Talent Acquisition Manager (TAM):** Oversees all recruiters; earns bonuses on team placement volume/GM.
- **SDR:** Sales Development Representative — outbound prospecting and meeting booking for AEs; KPI-based bonuses.
- **BDR:** Business Development Representative — similar to SDR, may focus on specific channels/inbound; also KPI-based.
- **Sourcer / Sourcing Coordinator:** Finds and initially contacts candidates before handing to a Recruiter; compensated on campaign metrics.
- **CS / Client Success Associate:** Manages post-placement client relationships to reduce churn and find upsells (internally called the "Deal Success" team).
- **Recruiting Ops Lead/Specialist:** Manages the operational systems supporting recruiting (e.g. Ashby hygiene); KPI-based commission.
- **IT:** Maintains, develops, and troubleshoots Scale Army's technology systems and integrations.

## Commission & compensation terms

- **Compensation (Comp):** The umbrella term for total pay — base, bonuses, and variable pay.
- **Net Compensation (NTC):** What a talent actually receives after taxes/benefits/deductions.
- **Variable Compensation:** Any non-fixed pay tied to performance (commissions, bonuses, incentives).
- **Commission Plan:** The documented rules for how/when a role earns commission (rates, triggers, retention gates, timing).
- **Commission Calculator:** A role-specific sheet calculating commission owed from inputs (placements, GM, KPIs).
- **Commission Tracker:** A role-specific sheet auto-populated via IMPORTRANGE from the All Sales Database, reviewed monthly by Finance for payroll prep.
- **IMPORTRANGE:** The Google Sheets formula pulling data from one spreadsheet into another automatically.
- **Payroll Prep File:** Finance's spreadsheet consolidating each employee's commission amounts, shared with HR in the last week of the month.
- **Payment Cycle 1:** First monthly payroll cycle — covers half of base salary, no variable comp.
- **Payment Cycle 2:** Second monthly cycle — remaining base salary plus any variable comp owed, processed via Deel.
- **Override:** An extra commission paid to a team leader based on their team's placements, on top of their own.
- **Kicker:** An extra bonus layer paid when performance exceeds a stretch threshold in a KPI-based plan.
- **Attribution Dispute:** A disagreement over who a placement is credited to in the All Sales Database, resolved by cross-referencing the Slack message, Ashby, and HubSpot.
- **Pro Rata:** A proportional calculation for a compensation event covering less than a full period.

## Tools & systems

- **Ashby (ATS):** Manages the recruiting pipeline — postings, candidates, interview stages, offers, hires; also used to validate attribution disputes.
- **HubSpot (CRM):** Tracks client interactions, deal stages, and pipeline data for Sales/AM teams.
- **Deel:** The HR/payroll platform for global contractor and employee payments; commissions load here after Finance's Payroll Prep File.
- **Stripe:** Processes recurring client billing — the largest revenue line on the P&L.
- **Scale Army Agents (SAA):** A separate AI sales-agent product line, distinct from the staffing/recruiting business, with subscription pricing and a human-in-the-loop operator role.

## Financial modeling terms

- **Scenario Analysis:** Building Base/Bull/Bear (or similar) versions of a model to show how outcomes shift under different assumptions.
- **Kill Switch Criteria:** Predefined thresholds that, if breached, trigger a recommendation to exit or halt a deal/project.
- **Steady State:** The point where a business/engagement reaches its expected run-rate after ramp-up.
- **Ramp Period:** The initial phase building toward steady state, typically with lower margins due to fixed costs spread over lower volume.
- **Breakeven:** The point where revenue equals total costs — often expressed as a headcount threshold or a time period.
- **Fixed Cost:** Costs constant regardless of volume (e.g. dedicated internal salaries) — relevant to operating leverage.
- **Operating Leverage:** How fixed costs amplify profit above breakeven and losses below it.
- **Unit Economics:** The revenue/cost of a single unit of business (one contractor, one placement, one client).
- **MRR:** Monthly Recurring Revenue — predictable revenue from active subscriptions/contracts.
- **Customer Risk Register:** A model tab listing clients with risk factors (payment history, contract terms, churn likelihood) and estimated impact if lost.

## Scale Army–specific terms

- **Scale Army:** The staffing and recruiting company (founded 2023), earning revenue via markup on contractor billing.
- **#onboarding-won-deals-msa-signed:** The automated Slack channel/message from Operations confirming a new placement/deal — the primary upstream input for the All Sales Database and all commission data.
- **Automated Slack Message:** The system-generated notification Finance uses (not the ATS/CRM directly) as its data source when a placement milestone is reached.
- **POD:** A small team unit — a POD Leader plus a group of AMs or Recruiters, managed semi-autonomously with team-level metrics/bonuses.
- **Referral Partner:** An external contracted individual/company referring clients, earning a % of GM on placements from those clients for a defined period (commission only, no base pay).
- **eNPS:** Employee Net Promoter Score — a KPI for certain roles' commission structures.
- **Dry Pipeline:** Open requisitions with candidates submitted but no placement made yet.
- **ATS Hygiene:** The accuracy/completeness/timeliness of Ashby data entry — a tracked KPI for Recruiting Ops roles, since poor hygiene causes attribution errors downstream.
