## Goal

Establish the process for preparing weekly and monthly financial dashboards — gathering data, formatting reports, and feeding the SA Business Health Report for leadership.

## Who this is for

Finance Team, Data Analysts, and Leadership. Process owner: Finance & Operations Associate.

## Process overview

Each week (or month), generate a Stripe deposits report and update the All Sales database with the period's sales data — status changes, fees, and adjustments from email/Slack notifications. The updated database yields key metrics (new hires, lost deals, deposits, buyouts, active clients), which combine with Stripe transaction data to populate the dashboard templates. The completed dashboard feeds the SA Business Health Report, which is reviewed and distributed.

## Step-by-step process

**1. Gather weekly data:** every Monday, generate a Stripe report of all deposits for the prior week (Monday–Sunday). Update the All Sales database from email and designated Slack channel notifications, making sure statuses, adjustments, client fees, and salaries are all reflected.

**2. Compile dashboard data:** pull key metrics from the All Sales database (new hires, lost deals, deposits received, buyouts, active clients) and extract the matching Stripe financial data for the same period, including detailed transactions.

**3. Populate dashboard templates:** input the gathered data into the dashboard's predetermined sections. For the monthly dashboard, aggregate data across the full month.

**4. Finalize the reports:** confirm every section is updated (weekly or monthly, as applicable) and that the SA Business Health Report reflects the newly populated dashboard.

**5. Review and distribute:** double-check data accuracy before finalizing, then share the dashboards and SA Business Health Report with relevant stakeholders.
