## Goal

A clear, fair, and secure process for managing and paying referral bonuses (external talent, clients, or internal talent), encouraging growth through internal and external networks.

## Who this is for

HR Team, Finance Team, Leadership, Employees, and Contractors involved in referring candidates or clients.

## Who can submit referrals

External Talent, Internal Talent, and Clients.

## Accepted referral types

- External candidate referral (for external positions)
- New client referral (from external or internal talent)
- Internal talent referral (for external or internal positions)

## Referral bonus structure

### 1. External candidate referrals (made by external people)

| Level | Candidate Requirement | Bonus | Payment Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junior | Monthly rate under $2,000 | $200 | Last payroll cycle of the month after the 90-day milestone |
| Mid-Level | Monthly rate $2,001–$3,999 | $300 | Last payroll cycle of the month after the 90-day milestone |
| Senior | Monthly rate $4,000+ | $500 | Last payroll cycle of the month after the 90-day milestone |

Bonus must not exceed 8% of the referred candidate's annual salary. Referrals must be submitted before the candidate has had any prior contact with Scale Army.

### 2. Internal candidate referrals (by internal talent only)

| Level | Suggested Bonus | Payment Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Junior Internal Hire | $100 | After 90 active days |
| Mid-Level Internal Hire | $150–$200 | After 90 active days |
| Senior Internal Hire | $200–$250 | After 90 active days |
| Manager Internal Hire | $300–$400 | After 90 active days |

Bonus must not exceed 8% of the talent's annual salary (may be reduced if it would). Managers may recommend candidates but cannot receive referral bonuses themselves.

### 3. Brand ambassador / influencer referral

| Referrer | Bonus | Payout Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Influencer | $100 | Last payroll cycle of the month after the 90-day milestone |

Paid in full after 90 days for the first 3 successful referrals; after that, 50% pays at 30 days and 50% at 90 days.

### 4. New client referrals

| Referrer | Bonus | Payout Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Internal or external | $1,000 | Paid once the client signs the MSA and the first search officially begins (kickoff scheduled or launched) |

Bonus must not exceed 8% of the client's projected annual margin. If no candidate is hired within 90 days of the MSA being signed, Scale Army reserves the right to adjust or revoke the bonus. (AE/AM/CS upsell referrals follow the separate $150 reactive rule in the Commission & Compensation Guidelines — see the [[commission-administration-sop|Commission Administration SOP]].)

## General rules

- Bonuses pay in the last payroll cycle of the month after the 90-day retention period is met.
- If the engagement is shorter than 90 days, a partial bonus may be considered for a successful short-term engagement.
- Managers may recommend candidates or clients but cannot receive a referral bonus for someone who reports directly to them — a bonus is allowed if the referral is for a different manager's team.
- All referrals must be submitted before any official contact is made with the candidate or client. No retroactive referrals are accepted.
- In case of duplicate referrals, the first submission takes priority.
- Any suspicious or unclear situations are reviewed by HR.

## How to submit a referral

Inform HR (Paulina or Ana) with: the referral's full name, position/role, client name (if applicable), and start date.

## How bonuses are paid

- **Internal:** via Deel as a bonus; HR approves and Finance executes payment once eligibility is confirmed.
- **External:** processed by Finance (Sandra).

## Safeguards & limits

- All referral bonuses require final HR and leadership approval.
- Bonuses process only after HR and Finance verify all conditions were met and confirm no conflicts of interest or prior contact existed.
- Suggested bonus values may be proposed (especially for mid-level referrals) but must never exceed 8% of the candidate's annual salary (candidate referrals) or the client's annual margin (client referrals).
- The program is monitored to prevent fraud, abuse, or conflicts of interest. Any attempt to manipulate, falsify, or take undue credit for a referral results in disqualification from the program and may lead to disciplinary action.
