## Goal

Define the process for paying vendors, ensuring invoices are verified, approved, and paid on time.

## Who this is for

Finance Team, Operations Team, and Vendor Managers. Process owner: Finance & Operations Associate. Supporting roles: Department Managers (expense approval), the Senior Operations Manager (HR, Finance & Compliance), and the CEO (final payment authorization).

## Systems used

Slack (expense request communication), Email (payment requests/invoices), [[glossary-finance-operations|Ramp]] (expense management and payments), Ramp Virtual Cards (operational tools/vendor payments), ACH wire transfers (vendor invoices), Google Drive (documentation storage).

## Process overview

The process starts when a payment request or vendor invoice comes in. Internal expense requests need manager approval via email or Slack. The Finance & Operations Associate reviews the request and submits it in Ramp; the CEO reviews and approves before payment executes via Ramp virtual card or ACH/wire transfer.

## Step-by-step process

**1. Receive the payment request or vendor invoice** — via email, Slack, or vendor invoice submission. Internal expense requests must include manager approval, the reason for the expense, the amount, and payment frequency (one-time vs. recurring). Requests without manager approval should not proceed.

**2. Review expense details** — confirm vendor/service provider, expense description, amount, frequency, and manager approval. If anything's missing, get clarification from the requestor before proceeding.

**3. Submit the payment request in Ramp** — including vendor name, amount, payment type, and supporting documentation (invoice or request details), then submit for approval.

**4. CEO approval** — Ramp routes the request to the CEO, who approves, requests clarification, or rejects it. Payments cannot process without approval.

**5. Process the payment** — once approved, Ramp executes the payment:

| Payment Method | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Ramp Virtual Card | Operational tools, software subscriptions, online services |
| ACH / Wire Transfer | Vendor invoices or payments requiring a bank transfer |

## Recurring vendor payments

Recurring subscriptions are typically processed via Ramp virtual cards configured for recurring billing. The Finance & Operations Associate periodically reviews these to confirm they're still active and necessary.

## Financial controls

- Every expense request needs manager approval.
- Every payment needs CEO approval in Ramp before execution.
- Ramp is the central platform for expense management and payment processing.
- Supporting documentation must be attached to every Ramp transaction.
