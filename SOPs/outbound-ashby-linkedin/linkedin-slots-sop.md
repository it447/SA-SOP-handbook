## Goal

Outline the process for managing LinkedIn job slots — posting new roles and refreshing existing ones — so postings stay accurate and tracked for visibility/engagement.

## Who this is for

Recruiters, Account Managers (AMs), Marketing Team, and Operations Team.

## Adding a slot on LinkedIn

1. **Log in and find an open slot** in LinkedIn Recruiter.
2. **Choose the job** — pull the JD for the role to post.
3. **Set the job details:** specific job title; Workplace Type = Remote; Location (LATAM, Egypt, or wherever fits); Employment Type = "Contract"; Job Function matching the role (Sales, Marketing, Admin, etc.); Skills — the essentials relevant to the role (e.g. CRM, HubSpot).
4. **Paste the JD** — make sure formatting is clean, no typos.
5. **Add client info** — pick industry tags matching the JD (e.g. Marketing Services, Printing Services).
6. **Add the tracking link:** go to Admin → Custom Tracking Links, copy the UTM tracking link, and paste it into the Application URL field.
7. **Test the link** — open it in a new tab to confirm it works before publishing (link includes `?utm_source=LIrecruiter`).
8. **Post it** — click Continue, review everything, then Finish.

## Refreshing a slot

1. **Log in and navigate** to the Recruiter tab → "View All Jobs" to see current slots.
2. **Pick the job to refresh**, close it, then click "Repost."
3. **Check the JD is still relevant** — update it if not, pulling the latest version or cleaning up the existing one; update the title if the role has changed.
4. **Tweak job details:** if the exact title isn't available, pick the closest match and adjust the corresponding Ashby job post title to match. Update Location as needed. Skills: up to 10, pick the most relevant.
5. **Add the tracking link** — same UTM process as above (Admin → Custom Tracking Links → "LinkedIn Recruiter" link → paste into Application field).
6. **Test the link** in a new tab before finishing.
7. **Finish up** — double-check everything, untick anything unnecessary, and click Finish.
