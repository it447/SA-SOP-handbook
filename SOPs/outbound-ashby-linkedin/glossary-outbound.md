- **LinkedIn Slots:** Job posts used to boost roles and increase the number of applications received.
- **Manual Slots:** Job posts manually created and posted by the operations team.
- **Automatic Slots:** Slots set up with predefined rules that let LinkedIn automatically post job openings on the job board.
- **Run a Search:** Going through the entire candidate database to identify a batch of candidates for current roles.
- **Ashby:** The Applicant Tracking System (ATS) used to manage candidate applications, track recruitment, and streamline hiring workflows.
- **Job Consideration:** A candidate being reviewed for a specific role — each reviewed candidate is a "job consideration."
