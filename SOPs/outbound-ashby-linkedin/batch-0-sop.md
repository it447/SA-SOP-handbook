## Goal

Outline the process for submitting Batch 0 candidate profiles to the client, enabling rapid alignment and an early check on how well sourcing efforts match client expectations.

## Who this is for

Recruiters, Account Managers (AMs), and Leadership.

## What Batch 0 is

See also the recruitment team's [[batch-zero-process|Batch Zero Process]] for how this fits into the overall recruitment SLA. A maximum of 5 candidate profiles submitted to the client within 24–48 hours of a search launching. It's a "calibration batch," not a final shortlist — a way to open discussion using real candidate profiles and confirm the sourcing approach is on track.

## Timeline

The assigned recruiter starts building Batch 0 in [[glossary-outbound|Ashby]] as soon as the search launches and is announced in the designated Slack channel. Batch 0 must be submitted within 24 hours.

## Guidelines

- **Timing:** presented to the AM 24–48 hours after the role launches on Slack.
- **Sourcing window:** candidates must have been sourced, active in a process, or applied within the last 3 months. If a candidate's last activity is older than that, the recruiter must message or call to confirm they're still available before including them.
- **Availability:** aim to confirm availability/interest for at least 50% of the batch, unless the candidate applied within the last 4 weeks.
- **Budget alignment:** most candidates should fall within the client's stated budget. One exceptional over-budget candidate is allowed (30% or less of the batch) with a disclaimer noting their compensation is outside the stated budget but their experience/fit warrants consideration.
- **Outreach:** every Batch 0 candidate must be contacted (message or call) confirming their profile is on file, sharing the role/budget, and asking them to confirm interest before the profile goes to the client.

## Setting expectations

Batch 0 exists to surface excellent, viable candidates early — not to hit a perfect, final shortlist. Some strong candidates may exceed budget or be unavailable, and client expectations don't always match market reality. Batch 0 gets early feedback before over-investing in a misaligned search.

## What Batch 0 is NOT

- A final interview shortlist.
- Mandatory for every single search — check with the AM if there's doubt about whether one is needed.

## Workflow

1. **Recruiter:** identifies/selects candidates, confirms availability/interest through outreach, assembles the Batch 0 list, verifies availability/sourcing recency/budget alignment, and flags useful context to Recruiting Leadership and AMs (region, role complexity, requirements, compensation) to improve future rounds.
2. **Recruiting Leader:** reviews the draft batch; if more than 5 candidates are presented, narrows it to the top 3–5.
3. **Ops review (Youssef):** double-checks the approved list before it goes to the AM — mandatory during the initial rollout period.
4. **Account Manager:** reviews the final list against the client's specific requirements, sends Batch 0 to the client, and follows up for feedback and direction.
