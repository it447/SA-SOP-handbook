## Company structure and entity separation (two separate Delaware companies)

We operate **two legally separate companies**, deliberately kept apart to protect each from the other's liabilities.

- **Company 1 — Scale Army.** Legal entity **PLTWG LLC**, a Delaware company, doing business as (DBA) **Scale Army**. Line of business: **talent, placements, staffing, and recruiting**.
- **Company 2 — SA Product Ops LLC**, a Delaware company. Line of business: **AI automation agents, software development, and coding**.

The two companies must never be commingled in client-facing paperwork. See the confidentiality firewall section for the cross-mention prohibition.

## Confidentiality firewall: no cross-mention between the two companies in client documents

To protect each company from the other's liabilities, **neither company may be mentioned in the other's documents**.

- The words **"Scale Army"** must **never** appear in any document for **SA Product Ops LLC (Company 2)**.
- Any reference to **SA Product Ops** / Company 2 must **never** appear in any document for **Scale Army / PLTWG LLC (Company 1)**.

This firewall governs **client-facing legal documents and agreements**. It is the primary reason the two entities are kept structurally separate.

> Note: This internal knowledge base is an exception to the "no mention" rule because it is an internal reference, not a client-facing document. Do not copy this document, or any content that names both companies together, into anything a client or counterparty will see.

## Arbitration and dispute-resolution policy (default, carve-outs, and referral exception)

**Default rule:** include **arbitration in every single agreement** for both companies — unless it is clearly and materially beneficial to us to omit it.

**Carve-out principle (how to draft the arbitration clause):** the contract should send **everything to arbitration except the things *we* would pursue the other party for**. Those exceptions — which we want to be able to take to court — are:

- **Solicitation** (violations of non-solicit)
- **Circumvention**
- **Nonpayment**

**Referral-agreement exception (when to omit arbitration entirely):** when we are **referring business to a new client** under a referral agreement, we generally **do not want arbitration**. The reasoning: we want to be able to **collect our money easily**, and we want the contract to provide that the **prevailing party recovers their legal fees**.

**How this maps to Scale Army's standard MSA:** in the standard Scale Army MSA, the dispute-resolution terms are a **legal lockdown** — removing arbitration or Florida jurisdiction is non-negotiable, and **Miami is the locked venue**. A **virtual mediation option** is negotiable; using a **different mediator** requires management approval.

## Client-origin and role-type restrictions

Applies to **Scale Army (PLTWG LLC)**. BDR = Business Development Representative.

- **Client origin:** New or startup entities are allowed if verified; **unverified** new/startups need review. **Sanctioned or reputationally risky clients** are prohibited. **BDRs must flag to Ops before proposals or Engagement Letters (ELs).**

## Part-time deal structure and guardrails

Applies to **Scale Army (PLTWG LLC)**. PT = part-time; FT = full-time; GM = gross margin; PM = project management; HR = human resources; IC = independent contractor.

- **Structure:** minimum **20 hrs/week**, priced **pro-rata + 10–15% PT premium**. Do **not** offer hourly rates below the full-time equivalent. Keep **GM ≥35%** and **margin ≥$600** even part-time.
- **Billing:** **pre-paid monthly retainer**. No post-use true-ups or refunds. Follows MSA § Payments.
- **Scope of work:** defined deliverables with capped availability. Do **not** take ongoing operational HR/PM roles under 20 hrs. Maintain **contractor status (Exhibit D)**.
- **Discounting:** **none by default.** Allowed **only if the client has ≥3 active full-time resources** — the **discount is client-based, not resource-based**. Do **not** combine with volume or bundle discounts. Log exceptions via **Ops #pricing-approvals**.
- **Reporting:** include **fractional status in MSA Exhibit A**; include hours and deliverables.

*Also asked as:* "What's the minimum for a part-time placement?", "Is there a part-time premium?", "When can a PT deal be discounted?", "How do we bill part-time?"

## MSA negotiation matrix — what is negotiable, what needs approval, what is locked

Applies to **Scale Army's (PLTWG LLC)** standard **Master Services Agreement (MSA)**. GM = gross margin; IP = intellectual property; PTO = paid time off; IC = independent contractor; ACH = Automated Clearing House; TCPA = Telephone Consumer Protection Act; CCPA = California Consumer Privacy Act.

| MSA section | Standard / open to negotiate | Requires management approval | Non-negotiable / legal lockdown | Context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Term & renewal | Renewal cadence (monthly vs. quarterly) | Multi-month commitments with exit clauses | **Auto-renew mechanism** | Monthly renew protects the company |
| Payment terms | ACH vs. credit card | 10-day extension to due date | **Pre-payment 5 days before start** | Must pre-pay before talent start |
| Fees & pricing | Margin structure per quote | Volume or bundle discounts | **Underpricing <35% GM or direct pay to talent** | Direct payment to talent = material breach |
| Late fees | Waive first late fee once | Remove interest completely | **Removing the clause entirely** | First-time waiver OK; clause must stay |
| Confidentiality | Add mutual clause | Narrow data scope | **Remove/limit confidentiality duty** | **10-day subpoena notice** required |
| IP ownership | Tailor Exhibit C for co-development | Shared IP in co-dev | **Remove IP assignment** | **Exhibit C mandatory** |
| Non-solicit | Adjust **12 → 18 months** | Lower the penalty | **Remove the clause** | **$75k liquidated damages fixed** |
| Buy-outs | Split fee into installments | Minor fee adjust on bulk buy-outs | **Remove the 12-month rule/formula** | Fixed formula (see numeric guardrails) |
| Termination | Clarify notice start date | Waive termination fee for short trials | **Alter the 12-month credit reuse** | Credit reuse locked 12 months |
| Liability | Add mutual indemnity | Expand scope | **Change the liability cap (1 month fee)** | Cap locked for legal safety |
| Telemarketing | Clarify data compliance | Add client indemnity | **Remove the compliance clause** | Mandatory TCPA/CCPA coverage |
| Dispute resolution | Virtual mediation option | Different mediator | **Remove arbitration / Florida jurisdiction** | **Miami venue locked** |
| Time-off (Exhibit D) | Adjust PTO days to match client | Change accrual after 90 days | **Change the IC nature** | Must stay independent contractor |

## Key numeric guardrails and fixed figures (Scale Army MSA and pricing)

Applies to **Scale Army (PLTWG LLC)**. GM = gross margin. These are the fixed numbers that recur across pricing and MSA negotiation.

- **Minimum gross margin:** **≥35% GM** on essentially all deals (existing, new, and part-time).
- **Higher GM threshold for discounts:** discounts generally require **GM ≥40%**.
- **Minimum margin floor:** **≥$600** margin (new-client intro offers and part-time deals).
- **Pricing floor definition:** under-floor = **below 2× salary** or a **spread of <$1,000**; below-floor requires **VP/COO sign-off**.
- **Volume discount:** **≤5%** for batches of **≥10 placements**.
- **Intro discount:** **max 10%** for the **first 2 hires** (new clients).
- **Part-time premium:** **+10–15%** over pro-rata; minimum **20 hrs/week**.
- **Payment timing:** **pre-payment 5 days before talent start**; existing-client tolerance Net 15–30.
- **Non-solicit:** default **12 months**, adjustable to **18 months**; **$75,000 liquidated damages (fixed)**.
- **Liability cap:** **one month's fee (locked)**.
- **Credit reuse expiry:** **12 months (locked)**.
- **Confidentiality/subpoena notice:** **10 days**.
- **Buy-out formula (verbatim from source):** "**Fixed: $7k / <3k or 19.5% annual ≥3k.**" See "Open / unresolved" — the exact meaning of this shorthand is not spelled out in the source.

## Key terms and definitions (glossary)

- **PLTWG LLC (DBA Scale Army):** Company 1, Delaware entity, the talent/placements/staffing/recruiting business.
- **SA Product Ops LLC:** Company 2, Delaware entity, the AI automation / software development / coding business; staff use saproductops.com email.
- **MSA (Master Services Agreement):** Scale Army's standard client contract; sections and their negotiability are in the MSA negotiation matrix.
- **GM (Gross Margin):** core pricing guardrail; **≥35%** minimum, **≥40%** for most discounts.
- **Floor / under-floor:** pricing below **2× salary** or with a **<$1,000 spread**; requires VP/COO sign-off.
- **Buy-out:** client fee to convert/hire talent directly; fixed formula, 12-month rule (verbatim figure in numeric guardrails).
- **Non-solicit:** anti-poaching clause; 12–18 months; **$75k** liquidated damages.
- **Credit reuse:** applying credit from a prior terminated account, valid **≤12 months**.
- **Exhibits:** **Exhibit A** = fractional/hours & deliverables; **Exhibit C** = IP / co-development (mandatory); **Exhibit D** = independent-contractor status and time-off.
- **§14 of MSA:** licensed/regulated-role clause where the **client assumes liability**.
- **Deel:** default platform for engaging/paying talent.
- **IC (Independent Contractor):** talent status that must be preserved (Exhibit D).
- **TCPA / CCPA:** telemarketing compliance obligations that must stay in the MSA.
